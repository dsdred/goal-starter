### Why this is a design task and not a small edit

Rev 1 identified three pressures. Two are dissolved and one survives — under rev 4's Icon-only model, for reasons that differ from rev 3's and from the same-day C2a text's:

1. **Storage + portable format (rev 1: a fourth collection and a version bump).** **Dissolved, and still dissolved under rev 4.** The dissolution never depended on the value set being fixed, only on there being **no category entity** — and rev 4 has no category entity either, plus a second, independent reason: the icon's vocabulary is a **built-in list shipped in the binary**, not data, so it needs no collection either. What the design adds is therefore **two optional scalar fields on the entity that already exists** (`ModelEntry.Icon`, `ModelEntry.Description`), with no new collection, no repository-interface change beyond `ModelPatch`, and no new endpoint; D5 accordingly proposes **no schema bump**, on ADR 013's own ratified precedent. The portable half was already closed by Q9 (FD-5 restates it): the initial scope touches no bundle byte, and transfer of the new fields is designed separately — under rev 4 that includes the icon, whose local-only status D11 states explicitly because a stable identifier is exactly the field a reader would assume transfers safely.
2. **The icon substrate (rev 1: no upload surface exists).** **Dissolved, and rev 4 restores the part rev 4-C2a had removed: something icon-related is now stored.** Rev 1 asked for a user-supplied image; Q6 closed that in favor of built-in glyphs, and the Icon-only decision keeps Q6 verbatim while making the glyph a **per-service choice** rather than a consequence of a category. So the persistence surface is **one bounded string**: `icon_id`, validated on write **by set membership** against the built-in library (`""` = neutral default), with still **no upload handler, no file path, no URL, no blob and no external icon source** anywhere (§Evidence rows "no upload substrate"; CD-5 survives as that ruling). This is the one place rev 4 **reverses** its own C2a text rather than extending it, and the reversal is deliberate: C2a rejected storing an identifier because a category identifier would have indexed **user data**; `icon_id` indexes **the product's own trusted library**, which is why FD-4's withdrawn «stable identifier on the service» clause returns for this field alone (D6). The security consequence moves back with it: the rendered icon's safety rests on **validate-then-escape** (membership first, `esc` second), while the **description** — the field that *is* arbitrary user text — rests on escaping alone (D6, §Test obligations 2 and 10). Because the identifier is persisted, its vocabulary is a **contract, not copy**: names are stable and append-only, the **supported** set is never reduced (§D6 as amended 2026-10-05 — a retired identifier leaves the picker and keeps its artwork, its labels and its write acceptance), and degrade-to-neutral is reserved for the **unset** and the **genuinely unknown** value — never for a supported one. D8's never-repair rule is a **read-path** rule: a later save re-serializes the file, so what is guaranteed through it is the field's **value**, not its bytes (D8, obligation 3).
3. **Maintained browser-acceptance selectors (R1 + R4).** **Still live, and now decided.** The suite binds to the models view in two different ways, and the difference matters for what the rename can break. Card bindings are `.model-row`-class-dependent and must be retargeted when services get their own family: **13 `#model-list .model-row` selector occurrences** — `tests/browser/core.cjs:71,97,236,293`, `migration.cjs:128,131`, `orphan.cjs:135,137,143,147,179`, `stress.cjs:143`, `validation.cjs:195` — **plus 2 inner lookups in the same family** (`validation.cjs:198` `.model-row-name`, `:200` `.model-row-title`), i.e. **15 sites across five suites**. Selector-plus-text bindings are narrower than they look: `core.cjs:75,109` and `validation.cjs:104,153` click `#view-models button:has-text("Добавить")` / `("Add")`, which is `models.add` («Добавить» / "Add", `index.html:301`) — a value this design does **not** rename — and `i18n.cjs:214,221` read `#view-models h1` but compare it against `t('models.title')`, so they are **value-agnostic**: they pass whatever the title says and **no maintained test pins the section title literally**. `.model-row` is **shared** with the Pipelines list, which emits the same markup from `renderPipelines()` (`app.js:764`, `:790`) and is asserted through it at **26 `#pipeline-list .model-row` sites** in `pipeline.cjs` (`:189,318,319,348,371,379,412,419,438,439,443,523,524,567,568,621,658,660,663,689,693,800,806,810,822,928`, of which `:189,318-319,663,689` are the load-bearing ones). D9's class-family question is answered by **Q7** (separate classes; Pipelines untouched), so that 26-site block is a **byte-identical** regression surface rather than an open choice, and the cards slice stays gated on §Visual contract.

## Scope

**In scope:** the section's product vocabulary; the **contract for an optional presentation icon** — that it is a stable `icon_id` chosen from a **built-in, trusted SVG library**, validated on write against the **supported** set, stored as that identifier, rendered as the card's **own** glyph for every supported id (chosen or retired-but-supported) and as the neutral default for `""` and for any unknown identifier, treated as an **append-only persisted contract with two memberships — Supported ⊇ Pickable** (D6, as amended 2026-10-05), and used for **nothing** but the glyph on the card; the contract for an optional free-text **description** (verbatim storage, 2000-code-point write bound, escaping, ⓘ display); the widening of the **existing Services search** to match Name and Description (R8); two optional fields on the existing service entity; the service card markup; the ⓘ info card and its secrets-safe content rules; forward/backward compatibility of stored records and of the portable bundle; the launch-**and**-presentation neutrality guarantee; the test and documentation obligations of the resulting slices. *(Rev 4's C2a text said "the contract for an optional free-text category field — how it is normalized on write, how it is filtered, how it is displayed" here. Normalization is **out** — nothing is normalized; filtering is **out** — there is no category filter; the category field is **out** altogether. Search is **in**, because the Owner's SEARCH ruling added a requirement rather than preserving one.)*

**Non-goals (explicitly out of scope):**
- **Category — as a field, a filter, a suggestion list, a taxonomy or an axis of any kind — is not part of this design.** The Owner's decision of 2026-10-04 removed it from the current scope, and the same message states the limit of that removal: «Это НЕ означает, что Category запрещена навсегда». **This bullet is deliberately *not* a restatement of rev 3's prohibition** — rev 3 declared "user-defined categories, in any form" out of scope, and **CD-11 explicitly withdrew that wording** («Явно снять/переписать прежние решения, которые запрещали user-defined category "in any form"») before the Icon-only decision removed the subject itself. What remains out of scope is every shape of the idea **for this design**, without a standing ban: no field, no filter, no suggestions, no taxonomy, no CRUD, no `Category` entity, no fourth collection, no `/api/v1/categories`. A future need to organize a large catalog returns as **its own additive design revision** under the conditions D3 records — and because nothing at HEAD stores a category, that revision would still strand zero rows.
- **User-supplied icon images** — the Owner's ruling is a closed list of exclusions, not a style preference: **no uploaded images, no file paths, no URLs, no blobs, no external icon sources; only the trusted built-in SVG library.** This is where rev 4's C2a text and the Icon-only decision genuinely differ: under C2a no icon field existed at all, so uploads were merely irrelevant; here an icon **is** persisted, so the exclusion is an **active contract** and Q6's original ruling («built-in glyph set; no upload; permit a later extension without implementing one») returns to full force. CD-5 survives as the same ruling in the same words.
- **A first-class "Application" term** — CD-10: **Service** is the umbrella word in this project's English UI copy and documentation. "Application" is not introduced as a competing entity name, and "Service Type" is not a synonym for Category (it denotes the runtime/executable kind). *(The non-goal keeps its wording, and its reason moves: under C2a it existed because a free-text category invited reviewers to call the field "application type"; under rev 4 the same temptation attaches to `icon_id` — a service shown with a database glyph is not a "database service".)*
- **A Service Kind / Workload Contract** — CD-4, generalized by the Owner into the **non-semantics rule** (D12): nothing in this design derives readiness, health checks, ports, restart policy, dependency semantics, executable behaviour, filtering, grouping, sorting, searching, counting, automatic labels or badges from an `icon_id` or a description. If GoAl ever needs execution semantics that depend on what a service *is*, that is a **separate architectural decision with its own ADR**, not an extension of D4/D6. *(Rev 4's sharpened reason: an `icon_id` is a **whitelisted, stable, machine-readable identifier** — exactly the thing a future implementer might be tempted to branch on — so the prohibition is now stated as a rule with a test, not as a consequence of the field's opacity.)*
- **The concrete icon library — which identifiers exist, which of them are pickable, what they look like, how many there are, and which control realizes the picker** — is a **Slice B output agreed against the visual artifact and browser acceptance**, not an ADR output (the same CL-1/CL-6 posture C2a recorded for suggestions, now applied to a library that *is* persisted). This record names no identifier and precludes none; what it binds is the **rules** the membership must obey once chosen — stable, append-only, never renamed, never re-used with a different meaning, **never removed from the supported set**, every supported id keeping its artwork and both of its RU/EN labels, retirement removing **only picker availability**, and an unknown value degrading to the neutral default without being repaired on read (D6, D8 as amended 2026-10-05). The **neutral/default glyph is the one exception**: it is mandatory, because `""` is a legal and common value from the first slice.
- **Pipelines** — R1–R5 speak about services. Under **Q7** the Pipelines list is a *regression surface* only, and an intentionally decoupled one: services move to their own card class family, so Pipelines keep `.model-row` (`app.js:790`) byte-unchanged and the 26 `#pipeline-list .model-row` selector sites must pass unmodified (§D9, §Test obligation 9).
- **Secret / credential storage** — R5 is a *display* rule. Model environment values stay stored exactly as today; ADR 006 remains the open direction. The description field is **not** a secret store and the ⓘ card renders it as ordinary user text, which is why §Test obligation 6 asserts a sentinel env value never appears in the card DOM even when a user pastes one into a description-shaped string by hand.
- **GoAl Variables / path portability** — ADR 018 D8 keeps them deferred; nothing in this design needs a path.
- **Server-side filtering, pagination, search ranking** — the catalog is a local, fully loaded list. **Rev 4 withdraws the filtering half of this line's subject entirely** (there is no category filter), and the search half stays true for the widened predicate: R8's change is **one comparison inside the existing client-side `filter()`** (`app.js:528`), with no server involvement, no query API, no relevance ranking, and no new endpoint.
- **Launch / lifecycle semantics** — R7 forbids influence, ADR 015 stays **FROZEN**, and nothing under `internal/process/` is touched. **CD-4 makes this non-goal load-bearing rather than incidental, and rev 4's model makes it more so than C2a did**: a stable, machine-readable `icon_id` is *easier* to branch on by mistake than free text (`if icon == "comfyui" { port = 8188 }` is a plausible-looking line precisely because the value is one of a known set), so "an identifier must never reach a decision" is enforced by §Test obligation 4 and 5 rather than by the field's opacity.
- **Multiple icons, icon sets, or a per-state icon** — a service has **zero or one** icon: one `icon_id` string, not a list, not a themed set, not a light/dark pair. Nothing in rev 4 changes that cardinality, and the Q21 style ruling (single color inherited through `fill: currentColor`) already excludes per-state recoloring.
- **The forward-version storage guard** — per FD-6 this is the standalone task `SCHEMA-VER-01`, and **Q22 ratifies that it does not block the catalog**: this ADR neither fixes it nor waits for it, and it runs whenever the Owner schedules it (see D5's honest consequence).
- **Transfer of the new fields to another install** — per FD-5 designed separately (D11). Rev 4 notes only the asymmetry a future design should know: of the two new fields, **`icon_id` is the safer one to transfer** (both ends ship the same built-in vocabulary, so a bundle value can be validated on import), while free-text description transfer raises the write-path questions D11 lists — which is a reason about ordering, not a licence to design it here.

**RATIFIED (the Owner's Icon-only decision of 2026-10-04 — «DROP CATEGORY FOR NOW. The Owner's scope wording is quoted because it is the contract, not a paraphrase of it: «Category в ADR020 rev4 удаляется из текущего design… R2 category organization удаляется; R3 category filtering удаляется; Category field не вводится; Category filter не вводится; Category suggestions не вводятся; Category taxonomy отсутствует», and — the limit of the ruling, recorded so this bullet cannot later be read as a permanent ban — «Это НЕ означает, что Category запрещена навсегда».

- **What this deletes rather than annotates.** Three shapes have now been deleted from the same section on the same day, and all three are gone: rev 3's **fixed six-token vocabulary** with `internal/domain/category.go` (`type Category string`, six constants, `AllCategories`, `ValidCategory`); C2a's **free-text `Category` field** with its normalization contract; and FD-3's / C2a's **category filter** with its derived option list and chrome mechanism. Rev 4 states **no `Category` field on `ModelEntry` at all** — D4's two fields are `Icon` and `Description`. No suggestion table, no taxonomy, no closed set of category values, no category i18n keys.
- **What survives from every earlier layer, because it was never about the vocabulary.** The structural prohibitions FD-1/FD-4/CD-2 all reached by different routes are unchanged and still binding: **no `CategoryEntry` type, no `categories` collection, no repository-interface change beyond `ModelPatch`, no `/api/v1/categories`, no category service, no category management UI, no CRUD.** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:7`'s "simplified **4-entity** domain" therefore stays true — the one consequence on which rev 2, rev 3, C2a and Model B all agree, which is why D5's no-bump ruling (Q3) was never in play.
- **Why an icon is not a Category, stated as a rule and not as reassurance.** The Owner's formulation is the binding one: «Icon отвечает только на вопрос: "Как визуально выглядит эта карточка?" Он НЕ отвечает на вопрос: "Что это за Service?" **Не превращать Icon в скрытую Category.**» A persisted, machine-readable identifier is a *better* hidden taxonomy than free text ever was, because it is already normalized: a future slice could `switch` on `icon_id` without touching storage, and every reader would call that clean. So the prohibition is not implied by the field's shape — it is stated in D12 and asserted by §Test obligations 4 and 5. Concretely, in this design an icon **never**: groups, filters, sorts, counts, labels, badges, categorizes, or names a class of services; and no UI surface may aggregate services by glyph.
- **What the UI therefore must not do with the library.** The picker offers **glyphs with localized names**, not classes: choosing `postgres` marks a card, it does not put that service "in a Postgres category". No category filter is added (R3 withdrawn), no category badge is rendered, and — because the difference between a design that has withdrawn a feature and one that never had it shows up exactly here — **no empty category badge or placeholder is added now either** (§Visual contract: «Не добавлять сейчас пустой Category badge или placeholder»).
- **Why removing the category axis costs nothing at this moment.** At HEAD `f50b42bfd6d744e4817bd92bf74ed4cb3976fe4d` **nothing stores a category and nothing stores an icon**: `internal/domain/model_entry.go:7-17` carries **nine** fields (`id, name, runtime_id, args, environment, active, autostart_delay, created_at, updated_at`), and neither `category`, `icon` nor `description` is among them; both dictionaries hold zero category strings and zero icon strings. Withdrawing a category model therefore strands **zero** stored rows and needs **zero** migrations — §Context names this as the only moment such a change is cheap, and it is why the Owner's "for now" costs nothing to grant.
- **Re-entry, named so a future request is not improvised.** A Category comes back as **its own additive design revision**, not as a slice of this one and not as a by-product of the icon library. The conditions this record sets for that revision, so the wait is a decision rather than an accident: **(1) evidence of need at scale** — the reason rev 4 gives for dropping it is that the repository holds **no evidence** a category axis is needed (zero product-side category bytes at HEAD; the feature existed only as design text); **(2) zero migration** — a new optional field on the same entity, still no fourth collection unless that model is re-argued, D5's no-bump precedent intact; **(3) the icon stays non-semantic** — the new axis must not be seeded from, derived from, or aligned with `icon_id`, or D12's rule has been reversed by stealth; **(4) the card must not preclude it** — which is why §Visual contract requires the updated artifact to show a layout that leaves room for organization metadata later, rather than one that has consumed that slot.

That question no longer exists — this record defines no categories, so there is nothing to collide. The *technical* overlap is untouched and stays ratified on its own terms (Q1, Q2): the entity remains `Model`, and `models.*` keys / `#view-models` / `/api/v1/models` remain as they are.

### D4 — `ModelEntry` gains exactly two optional fields — `icon` and `description`; the API gains no endpoint *(section rewritten by rev 4)*

```
ModelEntry + Icon        string `json:"icon,omitempty"`         // a stable icon_id from the built-in library; "" = neutral/default (R2)
           + Description string `json:"description,omitempty"`  // the user's own ⓘ free text (R5), stored verbatim
```

- **`icon`, and what its name may not imply.** The value is a **stable identifier into the product's own trusted SVG library** (D6), nothing else. The Owner's exclusions are a closed list, not a style preference: **no uploaded images, no file paths, no URLs, no blobs, no external icon sources — «Только trusted built-in SVG library»**. Rev 4 reverses C2a's naming argument rather than inheriting it, and says so: C2a rejected an `_id`-shaped identifier because it would have indexed **user data**; here an identifier **is** correct because its only referent is a built-in library the product owns. What still rules out `icon_url` / `icon_path` / `icon_image` is that each advertises one of the excluded surfaces.
- **Create:** the body decodes straight into the persisted struct (`handlers/models.go:111-113`), so the new fields arrive unvalidated; **`ModelService.CreateModel` (`model_service.go:41-46`) is where both are checked** — the same place the name is validated today, and the reason is unchanged by three revisions of this section: it is the single boundary both write paths pass through. What "checked" means is deliberately **asymmetric per field: `icon` by set membership, `description` by length only.** **Patch:** `ModelPatch` (`repository.go:911-918`) gains `Icon *string` and `Description *string`; `PatchModel` (`:920-949`) gains one `if p.X != nil` branch per field, inside the existing `saveLocked()` + rollback discipline. **`nil` preserves, `""` clears** — for `Icon`, clearing means the **neutral/default** icon; for `Description`, it means no description. These are the same `*string` semantics `Name` already has, except that empty is a *legal* value for both new fields (`Name` rejects it at `model_service.go:53-55`).
- **Icon write-path rule — membership in the supported set, and that is the whole bound.** A non-empty `Icon` must be a member of the library's **supported** identifier set (D6, `ValidIconID`); a non-member is rejected **`400 bad_request`**. **Pickability is never a write condition:** an identifier the picker no longer offers (`IconPickable == false`, retired-but-supported) remains valid input forever, because the write set is a superset of the offered set (P3) — tightening validation to the pickable subset would silently invalidate stored user data and is not available to a slice. **There is no separate length ceiling for `icon`**, and the reason is stated so a slice does not add one: membership *is* the bound — anything not in the set is rejected regardless of length, so a second numeric limit would create a second contract to test and document for no added protection. Validation is **exact and case-sensitive**: `icon_id`s are identifiers, not prose, so `Postgres` is not `postgres` and is **rejected, not folded** (the Owner's exact-data rule — and here, what is *accepted* must be exactly what the library names).
- **The diagnostic names the rule and never echoes the input — a deliberate divergence, stated because the repository contains both postures.** `ValidateEnvPatch` (`model_service.go:67-70`) rejects an out-of-set action with `"got: "+op.Action`, i.e. it **reflects untrusted input into an error string**; `handlers/models.go:176-177` proves the opposite discipline is also the project's own (audit logs field **names**, never values). Rev 4 follows names-not-values: the message says the icon must name one of the built-in identifiers and stops there, because this field is reachable from an untrusted body at the API edge, an echoed value lands in the response and in logs, and no debugging convenience justifies defining a reflection path in the contract. §Test obligation 2 asserts the set through the Go symbol, not through an error message.
- **No write-normalization of any kind survives rev 4.** C2a's D4 said "exactly two things happen on the write path: trim, then the 64-code-point bound", backed by a ban list. **The trim contract dies with its subject** — its only purpose was category text — and the ban list is not carried over as a rule about a field that no longer exists. What is kept is the Owner's general principle applied to the two live fields: **`description` is stored exactly as sent** (leading and trailing spaces included; no case folding, no Unicode normalization, no translation, no repair), and **`icon` is stored exactly as sent *when it is a library member* and is not stored at all otherwise**. `icon` gets no trim either: a value with stray whitespace is not a member, so it is rejected, and silently trimming would convert a client bug into a successful write. §Evidence records that the project never persists a trimmed form today; rev 4 keeps that posture instead of extending it.
- **Responses:** `modelResponse` (`:19-29`) and `newModelResponse` (`:31-47`) gain both fields — neither is secret, and `:31-47` remains the only place that decides what leaves the process, keeping the env **keys-only** rule intact.
- **Update handler:** the pointer struct at `:136-143` gains two fields and the patch build at `:159-166` passes them; the audit list at `:178-196` gains `changed["icon"]` / `changed["description"]` (names only, never values — `:176-177`, `docs/API.md:105`).
- **Description bound — RATIFIED (Q20, Owner ruling 2026-10-03); the icon picker's placement is NOT.** Q20's ruling is «Категория и описание в форме сервиса; описание не более 2000 символов» — and rev 4 must read it in two halves rather than adopt it whole. **Its description half survives verbatim:** the description lives in the service form and is bounded at 2000 characters. **Its category half is withdrawn with its subject.** **The icon picker was never in that ruling** — on 2026-10-03 there was no per-service icon, so the sentence cannot have settled where one sits. This design *proposes* the same service form for it, on the ground that the wizard is the only place a service's own text is edited (`index.html:139-210`, Create and Edit sharing one modal per `app.js:1242-1244`), and records that as a **proposal, not a ratification** (§Explicitly NOT decided names the gap; slice B's contract must close it before any markup is written). What remains ratified is the bound: **description is at most 2000 characters**, enforced server-side in the **same service-layer call** that checks the icon (`model_service.go:41-46` / `:52-57`), because that is the single boundary both write paths pass through and a UI counter is not a contract. Three consequences are recorded rather than glossed:
  - **"characters" means Unicode code points**, so the check is `utf8.RuneCountInString(p.Description) > 2000 ⇒ reject`, **not** `len(string) > 2000` — a Russian description is 2 bytes per letter, and a byte check would halve the user's allowance for exactly the locale this project targets. `grep -rn "RuneCountInString" --include=*.go internal/` returns **0 today**: this is the project's **first length bound on free-text user input** (`Name` is bounded only by emptiness at `model_service.go:53-55`). The nearest existing shape is the hostname rule in `internal/webui/validation/port.go:37`/`:45` ("must be at most 253 characters" / "1-63 characters"), which is a **handler-side** check on a config value — D4 deliberately does **not** follow it here, because `handlers/models.go:111-113` decodes the create body straight into the persisted struct, so a handler-side rule would be bypassable by any future caller of the service.
  - **The rejection is a `400`**, with a bounded message naming the limit and the received count and **never echoing the text** (mirroring `handlers/models.go:176-177`, which logs field names and not values — and diverging from `ValidateEnvPatch`'s `got: %s`, per the bullet above). Truncating silently is rejected: it would let the user believe a description survived.
  - **Which wizard step** holds the two inputs was **not** part of the Owner's ruling — Q20 asked placement and bound together, and the ruling settles placement at the level of "the service form" and the bound at 2000. The design's proposal remains **Step 1 «Название»** (`index.html:145-150`, currently exactly one field), on the ground that a service's name and its presentation belong together rather than with launch parameters (Step 3 is autostart + env + args, `:190-210`); Step 3 is not touched. **For the icon picker the gap is wider than for the description:** neither its step nor its control is Owner-ruled, because Q20's sentence contains no icon — so slice B inherits two open choices there (step, and `<datalist>`-vs-another-control) where the description inherits one. Because these are layout details inside an already-agreed surface, they are **not** open questions about the design's contract; they are named in §Explicitly NOT decided and must appear in slice B's contract before any markup is written.
- **Zero new endpoints, one new validation helper — and its message is not allowed to enumerate the vocabulary.** `POST/PUT /api/v1/models*` already carry a full/partial `ModelEntry`, so no route, no handler and no repository-interface change beyond `ModelPatch` appears. Rev 3 listed "Zero new endpoints and **one new validation helper**" naming `ValidCategory`, whose error text was `category must be one of models, images, audio, video, tools, other (got "…")`; C2a deleted that helper entirely; **rev 4 restores the shape under a different subject** — a membership check over the built-in icon library (D6) — and keeps rev 3's *message* deleted: the helper reports the rule and never the accepted set or the rejected input, so an error response teaches a caller nothing about the library. Both rejections (unknown icon, over-long description) surface as `400 bad_request` through the handler's existing `apierrors` branch (`handlers/models.go:167-175`).
- **`domain.Model` does not gain either field** (see D12). `ModelEntryToDomain` (`model_entry.go:75-87`) is therefore **not** touched — a deliberate omission, not an oversight. The typed icon set lives **beside** the entry, following `InstanceState`'s precedent (§Evidence), never as a field or method on the launch-domain struct.

### D5 — **No schema version bump: v8 stays current**

`SchemaVersion()` keeps returning `8` (`repository.go:552`) and `writeUnified` keeps writing `8` (`:530`). No migration function, no load-branch change, no normalization, no test-corpus retarget.

**Why — the precedent is this repository's own, and it was ratified:** ADR 013 §D6 states "**No schema version bump** (stays v8)" with the proof that `load()` has one version-agnostic `else` branch above 6, `encoding/json` ignores unknown keys, and missing keys decode to zero values (`013-…md:101`), and the Owner agreed it as decision item (5) conditional on test-proven backward compatibility (`:214`). The git record matches the claim: `3347b2b` added persisted fields (`pipeline_entry_id`, entry `id`, `EnvPatchOp`) **without touching the schema literal** and *added a test that asserts no bump happened* (`pipeline_test.go:523` — `"schema_version = %d, want 8 (no bump)"`). No commit since `9f0ecf5` introduced the literal has changed it.

FD-4 asked this design to reconsider the need for v9. **It is not needed**, because the two reasons rev 1 gave for bumping are both gone: there is no new collection to normalize, and no new required invariant for a legacy file to violate — a v8 file with no `icon` / `description` is *already* a valid catalog state (every service showing the neutral default icon, every description empty), which is precisely the backward-compatibility requirement R6 asks for.

What no-bump avoids, stated as the measured cost it prevents: **seven literal `8` assertions in six test functions across five files** (§Evidence row above; the seventh is `cmd/goal/autostart_test.go:283`, outside `internal/storage/`) retargeted — and "re-target, never delete" would have been the rule; `tests/browser/migration.cjs`'s v5→current fixture chain re-verified (`:17` seeds `schema_version: 5`, `:94` asserts the result is `8`); and **eleven statements in six documents** (`LIMITATIONS.md:70` and `:105`, `CONFIGURATION.md:259`, `ARCHITECTURE.md:5` and `:12`, `ARCHITECTURE_RU.md:5` and `:12`, `USER_GUIDE.md:1064` and `:1066`, `USER_GUIDE_RU.md:1031` and `:1033`) that currently read "v8 is current" would otherwise go stale in the same slice. Under D5 they all stay correct **unedited**, and `ARCHITECTURE.md:7`'s "4-entity domain" stays true because no fifth entity arrives. Excluded from that count on purpose: the ADR-history table rows (`ARCHITECTURE.md:163`/`:165`, `ARCHITECTURE_RU.md:159`/`:161`), which are dated records of what each ADR shipped and are not falsified by a later bump.

**RATIFIED (Q3, Owner ruling 2026-10-03): schema stays v8, at confirmed compatibility.** The Owner accepted no-bump on the same condition ADR 013 attached to its own §D6 (`013-…md:214`, item (5): "conditional on test-proven backward compatibility"), and this record binds that condition to a named artifact rather than leaving it as a mood: **§Test obligation 1 is the proof, and it is a gate, not a nicety** — if a v8 file without the two keys fails to load, or an icon-set+described service fails to round-trip, or any of the seven literal `8` assertions needs moving, the compatibility the ruling was conditioned on is **not** confirmed and D5 returns to the Owner instead of being quietly overridden in code. A bump buys nothing here anyway: an older binary ignores `9` exactly as it ignores unknown keys today, because nothing checks the version (`repository.go:254-272`).

**The honest consequence, which this record does not smooth over:** with no bump the durable file carries **no signal whatsoever** that it contains catalog fields. An older binary will read `icon` / `description` into nothing and rewrite the file without them — silently, with no version marker even to warn about. That is not a new class of risk introduced here (it is exactly what `3347b2b` already did to `pipeline_entry_id`), and a version guard would not have caught it either, because the hazard is *unknown fields*, not a *higher number*. The related guard problem — a file whose `schema_version` exceeds the binary's own — is therefore kept as the **standalone task `SCHEMA-VER-01`** per FD-6, with two options it must decide (fail-closed guard mirroring `portable.go:130-132`, or documented acceptance of the downgrade plus a WARN). **RATIFIED (Q22, Owner ruling 2026-10-03): `SCHEMA-VER-01` does not block the catalog.** The guard is scheduled on its own merits, before or after ADR 020, and no catalog slice may absorb it or wait on it. This is the correct ordering rather than a convenience: the guard's own subject (a *higher version number*) is not what the catalog changes, so gating the catalog on it would couple two unrelated decisions and would still leave the unknown-*field* case uncovered (`SCHEMA-VER-02`).

### D6 — The icon library is a **persisted, append-only contract with two memberships — Supported and Pickable**: built-in SVG, stable `icon_id`s, mandatory neutral default *(section rewritten by rev 4; the two-membership model added by the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05)*

An icon is **a glyph the user picks for their card, identified by a stable identifier the product owns**. Rev 4's C2a text said "an icon is presentation selected from UI copy, never a property of a stored value" — **that sentence is withdrawn, because the model now stores an icon property**. What does not move is the boundary the Owner drew around it: the library is **built-in and trusted**, the stored value is **only** an identifier into it, and the identifier is **only** ever used to select a glyph (D12).

- **What is stored, and where the set lives: one table, two projections.** `ModelEntry.Icon` holds an `icon_id`; the vocabulary itself ships in the binary, and it is **one** table carrying **two** memberships rather than two lists: **Supported** — the persisted compatibility contract, every identifier the product accepts on write and renders — and **Pickable** — the subset offered to the user for new choices, always contained in Supported (`Pickable ⊆ Supported`, P1). The design follows the repository's existing closed-set precedent rather than inventing a shape: a Go-side file beside `ModelEntry` — `internal/domain/icon.go`, `type IconID string` with one constant per glyph, `SupportedIconIDs()` and `PickableIconIDs()`, `ValidIconID` (membership in **Supported** — the write rule) and `IconPickable` (membership in **Pickable** — a picker rule and **never** a write rule) — patterned on `InstanceState` (`internal/domain/instance.go:9-21`) and `OwnerKind` (`launch_owner.go:4-9`), exactly as §Evidence records that project does (**the persisted field stays a plain `string`; the typed set lives beside it, and `domain.Model` never carries either**). Rev 4's first draft named a single `AllIconIDs` slice in this place; the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05 replaces it with the two projections, and **no `AllIconIDs` symbol survives in this design** (obligation 2's sweep expects zero hits for it, and no third list, alias table or rename map may exist — P5). On the JS side the renderer reads a **service-icon artwork table beside `ICONS`** (the `SERVICE_ICONS` map, drawn in the same inline-SVG shape as `app.js:425-434`) keyed by **every supported id**, and the picker reads a **derived pickable list** (`ICON_PICKER`) built out of that same table, with the neutral/default entry as the single lookup's fall-through — reusing `iconBtn`'s trusted-markup interpolation (`:421-423`) as a mechanism, not its entries: `start`/`stop`/`logs` are action glyphs and are **not** selectable as a service's identity. Keeping one table is what makes P1 structural rather than aspirational: a pickable entry that is not supported cannot be expressed, so the invariant is not left to a test to repair after the fact.
- **Which identifiers exist, and which of them are pickable, is not an output of this ADR.** §Scope names the library's membership, count, artwork and order as **Slice B decisions made together with the visual artifact and browser acceptance**, and the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05 puts the **pickable subset** in that same bucket: rev 4 binds no name, precludes no name, and fixes no initial split between the two memberships beyond the containment rule itself (`Pickable ⊆ Supported`; the subset may be the whole set). What it binds instead is the **compatibility rules both memberships must obey once published** — because unlike C2a's suggestion list, this list is now written into user files.
- **A supported `icon_id` is a persisted contract: stable, append-only, never renamed, never re-used, never removed — RATIFIED as the Owner's compatibility ruling of 2026-10-04, and amended 2026-10-05 to name which set it governs.** The ruling is a list, and it is quoted as the Owner wrote it: built-in identifiers are «стабильны; append-only; могут добавляться; существующий id нельзя переименовать или переиспользовать с другим смыслом; удаленный visual glyph при необходимости может деградировать в neutral/default, но persisted id не переписывается автоматически». Concretely, for the **Supported** set: new identifiers may be added in any future slice; an existing identifier's **name is frozen**; its **meaning (which glyph it names) is frozen** — renaming `postgres` to `db`, or pointing `db` at a different silhouette, is a **breaking change to stored user data** and is not available to a slice. **Removing one is not available either, and that is the 2026-10-05 amendment:** the ruling's «удаленный visual glyph…может деградировать» clause named a possibility this model now forbids, because a supported identifier whose artwork is gone is a stored value the product can no longer render — the exact failure the two-membership design exists to prevent. So **the artwork of a supported id may not be deleted** (and P7 turns that prohibition into a failing test rather than an instruction). What remains available is **retirement**: `Pickable = true → false`, which changes what the picker offers and nothing else (next bullet).
- **Retirement is a picker flag, not a removal; degrade belongs to the unset and the unknown only.** Rev 4's first draft used the Owner's pairing clause — «удаленный visual glyph при необходимости может деградировать в neutral/default, но persisted id не переписывается автоматически» — as the **read-side rule for a retired identifier**. **That use is superseded by the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05, and the supersession is stated here rather than left to a silent rewrite: degrading a supported identifier to the neutral default is prohibited.** The surviving half of the clause is the half that was never about artwork — «persisted id не переписывается автоматически» — and it stays in force for every case in this design. So a **retired-but-supported** id keeps its **artwork**, its **RU and EN labels** (P4), its **write acceptance** and its **render support**; all it loses is the picker entry — the Owner's ruling states retirement as exactly `Pickable = true → false` and nothing more. A card carrying such an id continues to show the glyph the user chose, and an API client may still send it. **Degrade-to-neutral belongs to two cases and no others:** the value is **unset** (`""`, an absent key, explicit `null`), or it is **genuinely unknown** — hand-edited, or written by a newer binary (next bullet). The distinction is not cosmetic: a retired id rendering neutral would have the product misrepresenting a supported asset it still ships, and it would make a picker flag change into a silent presentation change to stored data — which is the class of thing D8 exists to forbid.
- **Unknown `icon_id` on read: not repair, not rewrite, not delete — and reading it never makes it supported. RATIFIED as the Owner's read rule of 2026-10-04, scoped to the read path by the ruling of 2026-10-05.** «не repair; не rewrite; не delete; отображается neutral/default icon; stored bytes остаются неизменными» — that last clause is a statement about **reading**, and the amendment says what a save does instead of letting the sentence over-promise (D8's read-versus-save split): the load performs no write at all, and the **value** the file holds still survives every later save. So a hand-edited or future-versioned `icon: "comfyui"` in a v8 file loads without error, shows the neutral default, and keeps that string. **An unknown value never becomes supported by being read** — it gains no artwork, no label and no picker entry on its own account, and nothing may invent them to make a rendering "work". What the round-trip contract requires instead is that the **raw stored value stays available** to the API response and to the UI (obligations 3h and 10), so that editing some other field can never quietly clear it. Rev 4 recovers this rule from Q19's *second* half (which C2a had left with nothing to do) and pairs it with `getRuntimeName`'s existing degrade-without-repair precedent (§Evidence) — with the cleaner answer: a stale runtime reference is user data that must keep displaying its raw value, while an unknown icon has a defined presentation (neutral) and a defined storage answer (keep it).
- **On write a value outside the supported set is rejected, and the asymmetry is intentional.** Read: never repair. Write: `400 bad_request` for a **non-member of the supported set** (D4) — pickability is **not** a write condition, so a retired-but-supported identifier stays valid input forever. A library change therefore leaves files holding identifiers the product never knew (hand-edited, or written by a newer binary), and the user's **next explicit write** of that field is the only thing that can change it. Rev 4 states the consequence rather than smoothing it: the neutral default is not a transient state a slice may "clean up" on load, and any code path that silently clears `icon` is a violation of D8, not a convenience. **The superset is now an invariant rather than an implication (P3): the set accepted on write is strictly at least the set offered in the picker** — `API accepts ⊇ picker offers`. A slice may not "tighten" validation to the pickable set; doing so would break stored data that the Owner's ruling protects, and the two memberships exist precisely to encode the difference.
- **The neutral/default icon is mandatory, and it is the common case.** `""` — the value every service holds from the first slice, since nothing at HEAD stores an icon and ADR 018's NEW-import path never sets one (D11) — renders neutral, and so does any value outside the supported set. It is **renderable built-in artwork and the single lookup's fall-through**, shipped beside the vocabulary rather than inside it: it is not an id a user chooses, so it carries no picker entry and no library label of its own, and `""` is never written into the file as an `icon_id` (obligation **11(b)** states that the default is a member of **neither** membership, and obligation **11(h)** is the assertion that would fail if it ever appeared as a picker option). Under C2a the default covered "most values"; under rev 4 it covers **all services until the user chooses**, so its artwork is part of §Visual contract's must-show list, not an afterthought.
- **No stored image, no upload surface — Q6 restored verbatim, CD-5 surviving as the same ruling.** Rev 1's `image` field and its `"<family>:<name>"` token grammar stay deleted, and rev 4 does **not** resurrect them under the icon's name: the field stores an identifier, never bytes, a path or a URL. Q6's original 2026-10-03 ruling — **built-in glyph set, no upload in the initial version, later extension permitted without implementing one** — is exactly Model B's library, so it returns to full force rather than being superseded again; the Owner's ruling for this model states the exclusions as a closed list (**no uploaded images, no file paths, no URLs, no blobs, no external icon sources**). Because nothing is uploaded, membership validation is *sufficient* security for this field, which is the property C2a's free text did not have.
- **RATIFIED (Q21, Owner ruling 2026-10-03 — «встроенные SVG-значки в существующем стиле», returned to verbatim force by rev 4 with no restatement needed).** Icons are built-in inline SVG drawn in the project's measurable style: `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`, filled path data, a single color inherited through `fill: currentColor` (`.icon-btn svg` at `style.css:258`, `.pl-act svg` at `:508`, both sized by CSS at 20–23 px rather than by the markup), shipped inside the already-embedded `static` set (`server.go:36`) — so no request, no font, no external asset and no `Content-Security-Policy` question enters the design (the package has none today, which is precisely why the existing markup renders). An icon is therefore a monochrome silhouette taking its container's color; no multi-color illustration and no per-state recoloring (see §Scope's zero-or-one-icon non-goal).
- **Security: validate-then-escape, and the two fields are not the same case.** Rev 3 justified the renderer's safety with «validated against a six-member set»; C2a replaced that with escaping as the only control; **rev 4 restores validation for the icon and keeps escaping for the description**, and says which rule guards which site. An `icon_id` cannot become markup because it cannot be stored unless it names a library entry, and its markup comes from the app's own table — never from the value (`esc` is not even the relevant control there; the relevant control is that no interpolation is keyed on user input). A `description` is arbitrary user text up to 2000 code points, so **`esc` (`app.js:394`) is the only thing between it and the DOM** at every site it reaches — the card line, the ⓘ body, the wizard field — and `safeId` (`:413`) stays mandatory wherever a value participates in an id. §Test obligation 10 exercises both halves; §Test obligation 2 proves membership.
- **Accessibility follows from the non-semantics rule, and the two roles differ.** The glyph **on the card** is **decorative** (`aria-hidden`, no label, no role): it conveys no category, no type and no state, so announcing it would advertise a classification the design forbids. The **picker's options** are the opposite case — each is a choice the user makes, so each carries a **localized label** (`models.icon.<id>`), rendered as a **literal** `t('…')` call at its site rather than composed from the stored value, which is what keeps it visible to `TestI18nDictionariesParity` (`server_test.go:73`, §Evidence) and inside the runtime raw-key detector's existing `models` prefix allowlist (`tests/browser/i18n.cjs:167`, hence **no `CATALOG-DEP-06` instance**).
- **Where an icon may not appear is settled by the platform, not by taste — and the constraint moved from the filter to the picker.** HTML `<option>` elements render **text only**; an `<svg>` child is not displayed by the browsers this project supports. With the category filter withdrawn (R3), this no longer limits a filter control, but it now limits the **icon picker**: if Slice B realizes the picker as a `<select>`, the user chooses from **text labels without glyph previews**, and a design that expects previews silently ships an empty column. Whether to accept that or build a custom glyph-preview control is Slice B's choice under §Visual contract (CL-6's posture, re-subjected to the picker); what rev 4 binds is that the ADR not be won by accident. The icon itself lives on the **card** (its own slot, sized by §Visual contract) and, if the Owner's contract asks for it, in the **ⓘ header**.
- **The card slot's exact size, position and overflow behavior remain the visual contract's**, not this ADR's (§Visual contract): Q21 settles *what* the icons are and *how* they are drawn; Q7's artifact settles *where* they sit on the compact card, and slice C is gated on that artifact. Rev 4 moves one item squarely into the artifact's hands that C2a only implied: **the neutral default's own artwork**, since under Model B it is the icon **every** service shows until the user chooses otherwise.

### D7 — **Search matches Name and Description** (new R8); the category filter is withdrawn *(section rewritten in full by rev 4)*

**RATIFIED (the Owner's SEARCH ruling of 2026-10-04, quoted as written — «В ADR020 rev4 добавить изменение существующего Services search», «search должен искать по: Name; Description», «Icon в search не участвует», «Category отсутствует», «Это часть ADR020 UX scope»; this replaces rev 3's static six-option `<select>` and the same-day C2a derived filter, both withdrawn with their subject).** Rev 4's D7 is no longer a filtering section. The requirement it carries is one comparison widened inside a predicate chain that already exists, and the honest summary of the two withdrawn filter designs is: **there is no category filter, and no replacement filter is added.**

- **The change, exactly.** `renderModels()`'s search predicate at `app.js:528` is today `if (search && m.name.toLowerCase().indexOf(search) === -1) return false;`. It becomes **name-OR-description**: a service is kept when the lower-cased query is a substring of the lower-cased **Name** *or* of the lower-cased **Description**. Everything else about the chain stays — the query is still read from `#mf-search` at `:522`, still folded there, and still **AND-composed** with runtime (`:529`), state (`:530`) and autostart (`:531`), so widening one predicate can only ever **add** rows to a result set, never remove them.
- **Folding happens at comparison time and is never persisted.** `toLowerCase()` is applied to the *copies used in the comparison*, exactly as the 10 existing sites do (§Evidence: none writes the folded value back). Neither the stored `name` nor `description` is ever case-folded, so a search for `сервис` finds `Сервис` while the card still displays what the user typed — the Owner's exact-data rule preserved through a search change. The function stays `toLowerCase()`, not `toLocaleLowerCase()`: it is what the file already uses at every search site, it handles both locales this project targets, and swapping the folder would be a second, unrequested behavior change.
- **An empty description must not match everything.** `""` is skipped, not treated as a substring of every query — JavaScript's `"".indexOf(q) === 0` is a real failure mode here, because every service at HEAD has an empty description and a naive widening would make every card match every query. The predicate consults the description only when it is non-empty. A whitespace-only query keeps today's behavior (it is not trimmed, and matching it follows the same rule as today's name matching, now also over descriptions); **no trim is introduced on either side**, consistent with D4.
- **The icon never participates in search**, and there is no category for it to participate in. `icon_id` is not a text field, is not indexed, is not searchable, and is not a filter (D12).
- **The filter bar does not gain a control.** `#models-filter-bar` (`index.html:303-315`) keeps the four predicates it already has — autostart switch, search input, runtime select, state select — and **no `mf-category` element is created**, in either the static or the derived shape the two withdrawn designs specified. Rev 4 states the deletion positively, because withdrawn markup-shaped instructions are exactly what a slice ends up building: **no `<select id="mf-category">`, no option-population function, no chrome `dataset` kind marker, no ordering rule for user values, no `models.filter.all` / `models.filter.uncategorized` keys.**
- **i18n consequences, including one that reaches outside the ratified rename set.** The no-match empty state (`models.filter.no_match`, `ru.json:43`, «Нет подходящих моделей.») is **reused unchanged** — it already reads as "no suitable models", which remains true when a description is what matched. The search **placeholder**, however, advertises a narrower feature than the code will provide: `models.filter.search` (`ru.json:38`, «Поиск...») plus its **markup fallback literal** `placeholder="Поиск..."` at `index.html:305`. Rev 4 records this as a **required value change in both dictionaries, with the exact wording settled in slice B's UX contract**, and names the governance fact rather than hiding it: **this key is *not* in Q17's ratified ten-key rename set** — its value moves because R8 changes what search *does*, not because the section is renamed. Q17's census (10 keys per locale) therefore stays accurate for the rename, and this additional change is attributed to R8 so a reviewer does not read it as scope creep. `data-i18n-placeholder` **is** collected by `TestI18nDictionariesParity`'s `attrRe` (`server_test.go:69`), so the key's presence in both dictionaries is gate-checked; the literal fallback is not compared against its value by any test (§Evidence), so it must be moved by reading, not by the suite.
- **Why this change is provably non-breaking, and how thin that proof is.** Three measurements: `mf-search`, `filter.search` and «Поиск» appear **0 times** under `tests/browser` — no maintained suite exercises the search box, so no assertion moves; **no `description` data exists at HEAD**, so no existing row-count assertion can be shifted by matching a field that is empty until slice A ships it; and the AND-chain shape is unchanged, so the widening cannot cause a service that matches today to stop matching. The honest limit: once descriptions exist, a hit that used to be name-only can now come from a description — **that is the feature** — and any future test that counts rows while a query is typed must state which field it expects to match.
- **No new endpoint, no server involvement, no ranking.** Search stays a client-side substring scan over the fully loaded list; §Scope's non-goal on server-side filtering, pagination and ranking is unchanged and now covers R8 as well as the withdrawn filter.

### D8 — **Values are never rewritten on read; nothing about catalog metadata blocks startup** *(section rewritten by rev 4; the read-versus-save split added by the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05)*

**RATIFIED (Q19's second half, Owner ruling 2026-10-03, kept verbatim in force; plus the Owner's read rule of 2026-10-04 — «Unknown icon_id при чтении: не repair; не rewrite; не delete; отображается neutral/default icon; stored bytes остаются неизменными» — which the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05 scopes to the read path).** Rule: **bound on write, never repair on read.** Whatever the load path finds in `icon` or `description`, it keeps, and the load itself writes nothing. Startup never fails because of a catalog **value**, no load-time validation of stored icons or descriptions exists, and no normalization happens behind the user's back. **The scope of the promise is now stated in the Owner's own terms rather than inherited from the heading: this is a rule about *reading* and about *values*, not about file bytes across a save.** A repository save re-serializes the whole file from the typed structs (§Evidence's re-serialization row), so a slice must never promise — or test — byte identity after a save; what must survive an unrelated write is the field's **value**.

- **Rev 3's headline case is restored by rev 4, with rev 3's answer.** Rev 3 asked what happens when a hand-edited file carries `category: "gpu"` — a value outside the six-member set — and answered "degrade to «Без категории», leave the bytes alone". C2a deleted the question (no set, so nothing is outside it) and left this rule holding only the over-long-string case. **Model B restores a closed-set read case**: a persisted `icon` naming nothing in the shipped library — a hand-edited `"comfyui"`, or an identifier written by a newer binary — loads without error, renders the **neutral/default** icon, and keeps that value through every later save. **A retired identifier is not in this list, as of the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05**: retirement removes a picker entry only, so a retired-but-supported value renders **its own glyph**, never the default (D6). No membership check runs on the load path; membership is a **write-path** rule only (D4).
- **What rev 4 deletes with its subject.** The category-shaped answers — an over-long *category*, a whitespace-only *category* classified as uncategorized, a value that "filters by exact match" — die with the withdrawn field. Both surviving fields keep the same posture with different mechanics, and each hand-edited shape a reviewer might expect a slice to "helpfully" fix has a stated answer:
  - **A description longer than the 2000-code-point bound.** The bound is a **write-path** rule (D4); it is not a read-path rule, so the load path does not truncate, does not reject, and does not warn. A subsequent **patch that omits `description`** preserves the over-length value exactly (`nil` = preserve, D4), and a patch that **sends** it is rejected with `400` like any other over-limit write — the file changes only when the user asked for that change. Rendering an over-long description (truncation, ellipsis, wrapping, tooltip) is the visual contract's overflow problem, not the storage layer's.
  - **Whitespace-only, leading- or trailing-space, mixed-case, emoji-bearing or newline-bearing description text** is **data**. It displays as stored and matches in search as stored; nothing trims, folds or rewrites it — D4 persists it verbatim and D7 folds only *copies* at comparison time. `""` is the one value with a defined read-side behavior, and it is a **search** rule rather than a storage rule: an empty description is skipped when matching (D7), because JavaScript's `"".indexOf(q) === 0` would otherwise make every service match every query.
  - **A catalog field with the wrong JSON type** (`"icon": 42`) is outside this rule's reach, and rev 4 says so instead of implying a guarantee it cannot give: `load()` unmarshals the whole file into typed structs (§Evidence, the `repository.go:254-272` row), so a type mismatch returns `unmarshal unified schema: …` and startup fails **for any field at HEAD**, catalog or otherwise. D8 is a promise about **values**, not about **types**; there is no per-field leniency to promise, and slice A must not invent one for `icon` alone. Explicit `null` is the benign case and needs no rule of its own: it leaves a string field at its zero value, which is `""` — the neutral icon and an absent description — and `omitempty` then writes no key at all.
- **READ versus SAVE, and the one HEAD truth this rule does not cover (Owner correction, 2026-10-05).** The two operations are separate, and the design guarantees a different thing about each. **READ:** `load()` performs no write — measured at HEAD, no `saveLocked`/`SaveUnified` call exists on that path (`repository.go:216-292`), and `NewJSONRepository` writes only to seed an **absent** file (`:141`) — so a load repairs nothing, rewrites nothing, deletes nothing, and cannot fail because of a catalog value. **SAVE (from Slice A onward):** `writeUnified` (`:524-556`) marshals the whole four-collection struct, so the file's *spelling* may change through it — key order, indentation, `\u003c`-style escaping of `<`/`>`/`&`, `omitempty` dropping empty strings, the nanosecond `updated_at` stamp — even when the user changed nothing else. What must survive is each field's **value**, which is why §Test obligation 3 asserts value preservation and never byte equality. **The HEAD truth, stated rather than implied:** before Slice A an `"icon"` key in `goal_repo.json` is **not a `ModelEntry` field** (`model_entry.go:7-17` carries nine, none of them catalog metadata), so a hand-edited key is discarded by `json.Unmarshal` and is **lost at the next repository save** — no restart and no repair mechanism required, whenever a save occurs. **This is not a defect of the current product**, as the Owner's ruling states it: «Это не defect текущего продукта: Icon еще не является текущим schema field». It is the same unknown-field exposure that already applies to `pipeline_entry_id` against an older binary, it belongs to the family registered as `SCHEMA-VER-02` (with `SCHEMA-VER-01` as the standalone version guard per FD-6 and **Q22**), and it is exactly what Slice A closes by giving the field a destination — which is why this record states the pre-slice behavior plainly instead of letting D8's earlier wording imply a guarantee that does not exist yet.
- **The no-repair precedent is the one rev 3 already cited, unchanged:** `getRuntimeName` (`app.js:317-320`) degrades a stale reference instead of failing and rewrites nothing; there is **no repair-on-load precedent anywhere in the repository**, and ADR 013's backfill (`013-…md:102`) applies only to *generated entry ids*, never to values a human typed. Rev 1's "one bounded `WARN` at load" is still not adopted (`slog.Warn` remains available in-package at `config_seed.go:30` if the Owner ever wants one), and `ValidateCrossReferences` (`repository.go:1321-1337`, **zero production callers**) is still **not** extended — under rev 4 the reason covers both fields: `description` references nothing, and `icon` names a vocabulary entry rather than another stored entity, so there is no cross-reference to validate. The unknown-`icon_id` rule is this precedent's nearest neighbor with a cleaner answer — a stale runtime reference must keep displaying its raw value, while an unknown icon has a defined presentation (neutral/default) and a load path that writes nothing — and, per the read-versus-save split above, what survives a later save is that field's **value**, not the file's spelling. That validator's unwired state stays registered as BACKLOG **`CATALOG-DEP-02`**, as debt independent of this ADR.
- **What the Owner's Q19 ruling settled, restated so the dated text above is not misread as live:** «degrade on read» and «stored data is not changed» were adopted; **«normalize-to-empty on load» was rejected**, and Model B keeps that rejection with two concrete things to protect instead of one hypothetical: a load path that cleared an unrecognized `icon` would destroy data the Owner's read rule names explicitly («не repair; не rewrite; не delete»), and one that trimmed or re-cased a `description` would rewrite *the user's own words* into what the product believes is tidier — the same forbidden class of silent alteration C2a's `databases` / `Databases` example illustrated, now with no category axis left for it to apply to. The paired assertion also survives as a pair (§Test obligation 3): **what the DOM shows and what the file holds are checked separately**, since either half alone would pass under the rejected posture — and for an unknown icon that pair *is* the test: neutral glyph in the DOM, original string in the file.

### D12 — Catalog metadata is **launch-inert and semantics-inert** (R7), proven by omission, and the `icon_id` may never be a classification axis

`icon` and `description` are persisted on `ModelEntry` and returned in `modelResponse`. They are **absent from `domain.Model`** (`model.go:20-30`) and from `ModelEntryToDomain` (`model_entry.go:75-87`), which is the single hand-written bridge from persistence to the launch domain and the only shape the launch path can read (callers `instance_service.go:40`, `:94`; `pipeline_service.go:455`; `handlers/models.go:383`). **Rev 4 restores rev 3's stronger half for one field, keeps C2a's answer for the other, and widens the ban over both.** Rev 3 wrote that "with a closed set, no category can carry a path, a URL, a markup string or a per-category image at all, so there is nothing for the launch path to misread". C2a declared that no longer true — **correctly, for free text** — and substituted escaping as the only control. Under Model B both positions are right, about different fields:

- **`icon_id` is a closed supported set again, so rev 3's structural argument returns for it.** What the product will *accept* as a stored icon can only ever be a member of the supported vocabulary — «На write icon_id валидируется против built-in vocabulary» (D4) — and nothing repairs it on read (D8). So no value this design wrote can contain `E:\runtimes\foo.exe`, `http://127.0.0.1:8188` or `<img src=x onerror=…>`, because no such vocabulary member is storable: there is nothing for the launch path to misread and nothing for the renderer to interpolate. **Membership, not escaping, is what makes the values this product wrote safe.** The one asymmetry the 2026-10-05 round-trip contract names: the API returns the **raw** stored value so an unknown one survives an unrelated edit, so a *hand-edited* value does reach the client — and obligation 10 therefore keeps the rule that this string is used only as a table key and in the picker's selected/dirty comparisons, never rendered as text and never interpolated into markup.
- **`description` is exactly what C2a's text said it was**, and its hazard is unchanged: arbitrary user text up to 2000 code points, which **can** contain a path, a URL, a port or markup, since a description naming a service's own port is a perfectly legible thing for a person to write. For this field **nothing about the value is interpreted, anywhere** — it is never parsed as a path, URL, port, template, flag or Markdown in the UI, in the service layer, or in storage; its bound is length (D4) and its only safety requirement on the way to the DOM is `esc` (D6).
- **No semantic decision may be derived from `icon_id` — RATIFIED as the Owner's CRITICAL NON-SEMANTICS RULE of 2026-10-04, which generalizes CD-4.** «GoAl НЕ принимает никаких semantic decisions на основании icon_id», and the Owner enumerates the forbidden uses in two groups: **launch-side** — «launch behavior; readiness; health checks; ports; restart policy; dependencies; execution parameters» — and **presentation-side** — «filtering; grouping; sorting; searching; counting; automatic labels; badges; Service Type inference; Category inference». The ruling supplies its own test sentence, which any future slice should be read against: «Icon отвечает только на вопрос: "Как визуально выглядит эта карточка?" Он НЕ отвечает на вопрос: "Что это за Service?"», closing with the prohibition that gives this section its heading — **«Не превращать Icon в скрытую Category».**
- **The presentation half is new, and neither rev 3 nor C2a had to write it.** Both earlier drafts bounded the icon's reach at *launch*; this ruling additionally forbids using it as an **organizing axis**. That is not a hypothetical temptation once the category filter is withdrawn — "group the cards by icon", "sort by icon", "count services per glyph", "badge each icon" are each a one-line change that rebuilds the withdrawn classification out of a decorative field. **The `icon_id` may be read only where it selects or displays a glyph** — the card's renderer lookup, the picker that sets it (including its selected-state, dirty-state and clear handling), the label lookup behind the picker's option text, and the ⓘ header if §Visual contract asks for it. No predicate, comparator, counter, classifier or label/badge generator may take it as input, and the mechanism the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05 specifies for testing that is narrower and honest: **no member-id literal may participate in a behavioral branch outside the authoritative vocabulary / artwork tables and the test fixtures**, while generic variable-to-variable comparisons (`option.id === entry.icon`, `draft.icon !== original.icon`) stay permitted because they carry no meaning. §Test obligation 4 asserts this behaviorally **and** structurally, in the shape this repository already uses for R7.
- **`description` keeps launch-inertness and inherits the presentation bans by the same logic, with exactly one named exception.** The Owner's DESCRIPTION ruling lists the field's allowed uses — «presentation metadata; launch-inert; local-only; отображается в ⓘ; участвует в Services search; не влияет на execution semantics» — so **search is the one behavior a description participates in** (R8, D7), while grouping, sorting, counting, labeling or type inference from description text is forbidden for the same reason it is forbidden for the icon: a field the user types freely is not a contract the product may branch on. If GoAl ever needs semantics keyed to what a service *is*, that is a **Service Kind / Workload Contract** decision with its own ADR (§Scope non-goal) and its own storage — not these two strings.

Asserted, not assumed, by §Test obligation 5 — the same "prove reachability" discipline the repository applies to `PipelineModel.AutoStart`, whose inertness had to be re-documented (ROADMAP P1, ADR 013 correction). Obligation 5 is a **grep over reachability plus a struct/bridge assertion**, which is exactly the shape that catches a future branch on the string: `domain.Model` carries neither field, so no launch-path code can read it even by accident.

Two of them are new subjects rather than rewrites: item 4 tests the **non-semantics rule** (the thing the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-04 actually forbids) and item 11 tests the **one-vocabulary-two-languages** contract the persisted identifier requires. 1. **Backward compatibility without a bump (R6) — this is Q3's stated condition, so it is a gate and not a nicety:** a repository file with **no** `icon` / `description` keys loads unchanged, every service appears with the **neutral/default glyph** and an empty description; a service carrying both fields round-trips through save/reload with both intact; and `SchemaVersion()` **still returns `8`** — mirroring the existing "want 8 (no bump)" posture at `pipeline_test.go:523`. The seven literal `8` assertions in six test functions across five files (§Evidence row above; one of them is `cmd/goal/autostart_test.go:283`) are asserted **byte-unchanged**, not retargeted. **If any half of this fails, the compatibility Q3 was conditioned on is not confirmed and D5 returns to the Owner** rather than being settled by adding a bump inside an implementation slice.

2. **The write-path rules on both fields (D4/D6), and the presence of exactly one vocabulary (CD-5, Q20):** **icon** — a member of the **supported** set (`SupportedIconIDs()` / `ValidIconID`) is accepted, **including an identifier the picker no longer offers** (`IconPickable == false`; pickability is never a write condition — P3), and anything else is rejected with `400`, and the rejects must include the shapes a real integration produces: a **case-variant** of a valid id (`ComfyUI` when `comfyui` is the member — the membership test is **exact and case-sensitive**, D4), a valid id with leading or trailing whitespace, and an unknown-but-plausible identifier. **`""` is accepted and means the neutral/default icon.** There is deliberately **no length assertion on `icon`** — the membership check is the entire rule, so a ten-thousand-character string must fail as an *unknown id*, never as *too long*, and the message must not differ in a way that discloses the library. **description** — **exactly 2000 code points accepted, 2001 rejected with `400`**, and the boundary proven with a **non-ASCII** string (a 2000-letter Cyrillic value is 4000 bytes, so a `len(string)` check fails the accepted half). **No trim, no normalization, on either field:** `"  Базы  "` persists exactly, `"   "` (whitespace-only) persists as three spaces and is **not** collapsed to `""`, and a description with interior double spaces keeps them. **Patch semantics:** absent **or explicit `null`** `icon` / `description` preserves the stored value; an explicit `""` clears it (icon → neutral, description → empty). **Diagnostics:** every rejection message **names the rule and never echoes the offending value** — this is a deliberate divergence from `ValidateEnvPatch`'s `"got: "+op.Action` (`model_service.go:67-70`) and an explicit match to the audit names-not-values rule at `handlers/models.go:178-196`; for the description it may state the limit and the received count, for the icon it may **not** enumerate or quote the library. **Layering:** the storage layer demonstrably performs no validation (both rules live in the service, like `Name`, at `model_service.go:41-46` and `:52-57`). **And the vocabulary assertion, which is the half C2a could not state:** `internal/domain/icon.go` exists and carries `SupportedIconIDs` / `PickableIconIDs` / `ValidIconID` / `IconPickable`, **exactly one** domain vocabulary file of this kind exists, **no `AllIconIDs` symbol remains anywhere** and no third list, alias table or rename map of any kind exists (the 2026-10-05 ruling replaced the single-list shape with two projections, so a slice that reintroduces one has built the taxonomy this design forbids), and `grep -rni "categor" internal/` yields **zero product hits** — no `internal/domain/category.go`, no `AllCategories` / `ValidCategory` symbol anywhere.

3. **Values are never rewritten on read, and survive every later save (D8, per ratified Q19's surviving half, as the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05 scopes it).** This obligation asserts **value** preservation, never byte equality: a repository save re-serializes the whole file from the typed structs (§Evidence's re-serialization row), so byte identity across a save is not a promise this design makes and not a property any suite may test. Where a case below says "the file still holds X", the check re-reads the file and looks for that **value** inside the re-serialized JSON. **The (DOM state + stored value) pair is asserted for every case**, because either half alone would also pass under the repair-on-load posture the Owner rejected. No `WARN` is required by this design, so none is asserted.
   - **(a) Unknown `icon_id` (hand-edited, or written by a newer binary):** loads without error, **renders the neutral/default glyph**, and the stored value is still there after the load **and** after a subsequent unrelated save. **A retired-but-supported identifier is the opposite case and must be in the same fixtures:** it renders **its own glyph**, not the default (D6 as amended) — a test that sees a retired id degrade is this obligation failing.
   - **(b) Unset shapes:** an **absent key**, an **explicit `null`** and `""` all leave the Go string at `""`, render neutral, and `omitempty` then writes **no key at all** — so a `null`-valued icon must not gain a key on the next unrelated save.
   - **(c) Absurdly long `icon`** (a 10,000-character value): survives load+save **untruncated**, because membership is a **write** rule and only an explicit write of that field may reject it.
   - **(d) Over-2000 `description`:** likewise survives load+save with every code point intact.
   - **(e) Markup-shaped `description` text:** survives **verbatim**, because escaping is a render-time concern only (obligation 10).
   - **(f) The read path performs no write** — measured at HEAD: `load()` (`repository.go:216-292`) has no save on it and `NewJSONRepository` (`:141`) writes only to seed an **absent** file. A test that merely opens the repository and re-reads it proves the **vacuous** half of this rule only; the non-vacuous half is case (a)'s "still there after an unrelated save", and the obligation is stated so a slice cannot satisfy it with the vacuous form.
   - **(g) The boundary stated honestly, because D8 is a promise about *values*, not about *types* or *spelling*:** a wrong JSON type (`"icon": 12`) still fails at `json.Unmarshal` and aborts the whole load through `repository.go:265-266` exactly as it does today, and nothing here changes that. **Byte-level spelling is explicitly not protected** — after a save the file may differ in whitespace, in top-level key order, in `\u003c`-style escaping of `<`/`>`/`&`, and in the nanosecond `updated_at` stamp; none of that is a change to a catalog value, and a slice must not test for it. The next **explicit** write of that service may set or clear either field through the normal write path, and that is the only way either value ever changes.
   - **(h) The browser-level round trip Slice B owes (the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05, stated separately because it is a UI contract, not a storage one):** with a service whose stored `icon` is an **unknown** value, editing its **Name** or **Description** in the wizard and saving must leave the raw `icon` value untouched. Concretely: the API response carries the **raw stored `icon_id`** (so the client can round-trip what it did not choose); the picker distinguishes **unset** / **known-and-pickable** / **stored-but-not-offered** and never presents an unknown value as if it were unset inside the edit state; the icon is sent in a patch **only if the user actually changed it**; and an explicit clear stays a **separate action**. A UI that shows the neutral glyph and then writes `""` back on the next save is this obligation failing — that is the silent-clear path D8 forbids, arriving through the browser instead of the loader.

4. **The non-semantics rule (D12) — the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-04, tested rather than trusted:** nothing in this design may derive behavior or presentation *decisions* from an `icon_id` or a description. Assert it in both registers. **Behaviorally:** two services that differ **only** by `icon_id`, and two that differ **only** by description, must produce identical list ordering, identical counts, identical filter results across every existing filter combination (runtime, state, autostart, search), and identical card text apart from the glyph itself and the description line; a test that changes an `icon_id` and observes a **list-order, count, state or reachability** change is this obligation failing. **Structurally — by the mechanism the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05 specifies, which replaces this obligation's earlier «no comparison of an icon value anywhere» rule: that rule was too strong to satisfy honestly, because a picker that works must compare icon values.** What is **PERMITTED** is presentation-mechanical handling of the icon as a value: the renderer's table lookup, the neutral fall-back, the picker's selected-state, a dirty-state comparison between the draft and the stored value, clear/reset, an accessibility-label lookup, and generic keyboard/navigation handling — comparisons of the form `option.id === entry.icon` or `draft.icon !== original.icon` compare variables to variables and carry no product meaning. What is **FORBIDDEN** is the **identity of a specific `icon_id`** driving anything. The structural test is therefore stated as a rule a suite can actually run: **no member-id literal may participate in a behavioral branch outside the authoritative icon vocabulary / artwork tables and the test fixtures** — no `if (icon === "comfyui")`, no `switch` over icon identifiers, no icon-keyed map feeding a port, argument, working directory, env key, readiness rule, filter predicate, comparator, counter, badge or label generator. Alongside it the suite asserts **P6 — library-agnosticism: adding a new supported or pickable identifier requires no new branch anywhere in `app.js`**; a slice that has to write one has just turned the library into a classification axis. On the Go side `Icon` is touched only in `model_entry.go` (field), `model_service.go` (validation), `repository.go` (persistence) and `handlers/models.go` (response). **Adversarially, in the same fixtures:** include a service whose `icon_id` is shaped like a **runtime name** and whose description is shaped like **configuration** (`--host=0.0.0.0`, `E:\runtimes\python.exe`, `http://127.0.0.1:8188`), and assert no port, argument, working directory, env key, readiness behavior, **automatic label or badge** changes. **And the inference half:** the card's text is derived from Name, runtime kind and state only — no badge, no subtitle, no `Service Type` wording and no group heading may be produced from either new field. A stable, whitelisted identifier is *easier* to branch on by mistake than free text was (`if icon == "comfyui" { port = 8188 }` is a plausible-looking line precisely because the value is one of a known set), which is why this obligation exists at all rather than being inherited from the field's opacity.

5. **Launch neutrality (R7), and the two fields provably never reach a decision (CD-4):** a service with an icon and a description produces a **byte-identical** `CommandSpec` to the same service with neither (assert at `LaunchResolver`/`ResolvePreview`), and `domain.Model` / `ModelEntryToDomain` demonstrably carry **neither** field — the omission is the proof, so assert it at the struct/bridge level (`model_entry.go:75-87`), not only by behavior. The adversarial half obligation 4 sets up is executed against **every** launch call site the §Evidence table names — `instance_service.go:40`, `:94`, `pipeline_service.go:455`, `handlers/models.go:383` — with the assertion that **nothing changes**: no added argument, no port, no working directory, no env key, no readiness behavior. This is the obligation that keeps ADR 015 **FROZEN** true in fact rather than in intention.

6. **Secrets safety (R5):** the ⓘ card's data path returns `environment_keys` only; a model whose env values contain a sentinel string never shows that value in the resolve response, the list response, or the rendered card DOM. **The new field makes the assertion sharper and narrower at once:** the sentinel must never appear anywhere in those payloads *emitted by the system*, and the description must never be a channel for one — so the suite asserts that no code path places environment data into the description or into the card's description slot. What the design does **not** claim, and the test must not pretend: a user who types a secret into a free-text description has put it in a plainly stored, plainly rendered local field, and the card will show exactly what was typed (D4's verbatim rule). That is a documented limitation of a local-only manager (`docs/LIMITATIONS.md`, §Documentation obligations), not a validation rule, and no slice may "protect" the user by altering, truncating or masking the stored text.

7. **ⓘ interaction (R5) — including the empty-description case this design makes common:** hover opens; click opens with no hover (keyboard-reachable); one card open at a time; ESC/outside-click closes; resolve is requested **once** per opened card and cached (assert request count, proving D10's no-fan-out rule); a 400 variable diagnostic renders instead of a generic failure. **Rev 4 adds:** a service with an **empty** description still opens a valid card — icon slot, name, environment keys — whose default/empty copy comes from a translated label and is never a raw empty string, never a stored value, and never the `icon_id` itself; and the card's header shows the **service Name**, so a stored identifier can never surface as user-visible text through this path.

8. **Portable, unchanged (R6, per ratified Q9 + FD-5, read against Q11):** export from an install whose services **all** carry an icon and a description produces a **valid bundle v1 with an unchanged shape** carrying **no** `icon` / `description` keys; importing it into a clean install creates services with **no icon (so the neutral/default glyph) and no description** — the documented loss; importing it over an install whose **same-id** service *does* have them **leaves the local `icon` and `description` in place** (D11's NEW-vs-UPDATE distinction, which rests on ADR 018 §D7/§D11 restoring field-by-field — `018-…md:112`, `:147`); **zero bytes changed** under `internal/application/portable/`, `internal/storage/import_plan.go`, `internal/webui/handlers/portable.go` and `tests/browser/portable.cjs` — the negative assertion is what proves the ruling was honored. TLS material and env values stay out of the bundle (ADR 019 §D25 / **its** obligation 12 at `019-…md:330`, not this record's obligation 12; ADR 014 D13) — re-asserted, not inherited. **Sequencing note this obligation carries:** the UPDATE half is only executable once ADR 018's apply path exists, which Q11 guarantees; until then the same-id case cannot be produced at all, because skip-existing classifies it `existing` and never touches it (`import_plan.go:144-218` per ROADMAP `:152`). **And one thing this obligation must not become:** D11 notes that `icon_id` is the *safer* of the two fields to transfer someday (both ends ship the same vocabulary, so an imported value is validateable on arrival) — that is a reason about a future ordering, not a licence for any slice here to validate, map or carry an icon across the portable boundary.

9. **Card redesign (R4, per ratified Q7 + Q8):** markup asserted against the **committed visual contract's numbered V- items** (§Visual contract) — never phrased as "matches the mockup"; the **15** service-side browser sites (§Blast surface) retarget to the new family and pass; RU/EN parity (`tests/browser/i18n.cjs`); **and all 26 `#pipeline-list .model-row` selector sites in `pipeline.cjs` pass unmodified** — under Q7 that file is *evidence of non-coupling*, not a file to edit, and any need to touch it means the ruling was violated. The **Q8 fix is asserted in the same slice**: after it, no element in `#model-list` carries a native `title` attribute (query the rendered DOM, both locales, with a service that has a description and an orphan one so `:564` and `:565` are both reached), that each of those two elements now carries `data-tooltip` with the same text it dropped from `title` (the swap must not lose the information), and that `app.js:795`'s pipeline `title=` is still present and untouched. **Rev 4 adds the Owner's explicit prohibition as an assertion:** with a service that has **no** icon and **no** description, the rendered card contains **no empty badge, no reserved category slot and no category-shaped class or attribute** — «Не добавлять сейчас пустой Category badge или placeholder» is tested, not assumed (§Visual contract).

10. **No new attack surface — validate-then-escape for the glyph, escape for text (D4/D6, restated for a persisted identifier):** the `icon_id` is **validated on write against the trusted built-in set**, so no stored value can steer which markup the renderer emits — assert that an unknown, hand-edited or absent id falls through to the **neutral/default** entry, and that no code path builds icon markup by concatenating the raw stored value (the id is used **only** as a key into the service-icon table). Every library entry's markup is **inline `<svg>`** of the same shape as the existing `ICONS` entries (`app.js:425-434`): assert **no `<img>`, no `src`, no external URL, no `url()`, no data URI, no file path** appears in the icon table or in the rendered card — CD-5's exclusion list checked in the shipped bytes rather than in prose. **No user text reaches the DOM unescaped at any description site** — the card's description line, the ⓘ body, and the wizard field — using `esc` (`app.js:394`), and the test must exercise a description containing `<img src=x onerror=…>` to prove that **escaping, not the length bound and not the icon's membership check**, is what protects the page. **Accessibility:** the card glyph is **decorative** (`aria-hidden`, no title/text of its own, Q21's single inherited color through `fill: currentColor`), while the picker's entries are **labeled** options — a control whose only text is the glyph would fail here. **The raw stored `icon` value is data, never text on screen** (added by the Owner's 2026-10-05 round-trip ruling): the API returns it verbatim so an unknown value can survive an unrelated edit (obligation 3h), so the suite asserts that the string is used only as a table key and in the picker's selected/dirty comparisons, never rendered as visible text, and escaped through `esc` (`app.js:394`) if it ever reaches an attribute — a hand-edited `<img src=x onerror=…>` icon value must become neither markup nor a label, and no fallback may print the identifier where the glyph is missing. **The suite asserts the *absence* of an upload surface stays true** (no `multipart` / `FormFile` handler appears in `internal/`), so a later extension cannot smuggle one in under this slice. **And it asserts nothing about CSP:** this design ships no CSP change, so no obligation may claim one.

11. **One vocabulary, two memberships, two languages, append-only (D6, as amended by the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05) — the parity invariants.** Rev 4's first draft required the Go list, the JS artwork keys and the picker's option list to be **exactly equal**; **that formula is no longer valid as stated** — the equality that does survive is narrower and is asserted separately as **P8: the picker equals the Pickable projection, not the supported set** — because the picker now shows a *subset* of what is stored and rendered, and a test built on the old formula would fail a correct implementation. The obligation is instead these invariants, each checked relative to the **Supported** set rather than the pickable subset, and each asserted by reading the shipped `app.js` and both dictionaries from a Go test on the `server_test.go:33` regex precedent — a parity break between two halves tested separately is the single most likely way this design fails: **(a) P1 `Pickable ⊆ Supported`** — structural in the single-table shape D6 specifies (`ICON_PICKER` is derived from `SERVICE_ICONS`), and asserted directly as well: no id is `IconPickable` unless `ValidIconID` accepts it. **(b) P2 `Supported == artwork keys`, both directions** — every supported id has renderable built-in artwork, and every key in the artwork table is a supported id; the neutral/default entry is the lookup's **fall-through**, shipped beside the table and a member of **neither** membership (it must exist on both sides and must never be pickable, and `""` must never be written as an `icon_id`). **(c) P3 `accepted on write ⊇ offered in the picker`** — a retired-but-supported identifier is accepted by the API (D4) although the picker never shows it; tightening validation to the pickable set is this obligation failing. **(d) P4 — the Owner's clarification of 2026-10-05, and the half the first draft got wrong: label parity is relative to Supported, not Pickable.** `Supported == RU icon-label keys == EN icon-label keys`, i.e. **every supported id** carries `models.icon.<id>` in **both** dictionaries — pickable **and** retired-but-supported alike — because retirement removes a picker entry and never a label, artwork, write acceptance or render support. The mechanism has to change with the rule and is stated so a slice does not quietly keep the old one: a retired id has **no literal `t('models.icon.<id>')` call site**, so `tCallRe` (`server_test.go:73`) can no longer serve as the whole-vocabulary guard; the parity test must **enumerate `SupportedIconIDs()` against both dictionaries** and fail statically on a missing key. Literal `t()` call sites remain required for the pickable entries, so the common case is still gate-visible, and the stored `icon_id` string never appears in the DOM as a label (obligation 10). **A retired label is not displayed to the user** — it stays part of the supported compatibility metadata, which is what makes it safe to keep. **(e) P5 no alias vocabulary:** exactly one entry per id and **no alias table, no rename mapping, no legacy-key translation anywhere** — the Owner's «Не создавай alias vocabulary» — because the only permitted change is **adding** an id (D6), and an alias mechanism would silently re-introduce the taxonomy this design forbids. This **replaces** the first draft's «remove a member from the JS table while a stored value still references it and see it degrade» scenario, which the 2026-10-05 ruling prohibits rather than tests. **(f) P7 artwork coverage:** every supported id renders a **non-neutral** glyph, so deleting the artwork of a supported identifier is not a silent visual regression but a failing test — which is how D6's prohibition on deleting the artwork of a supported identifier is enforced instead of hoped for. **(g)** The single `grep -rni "categor" internal/` sweep of obligation 2 is re-run here against the **shipped UI** as well as the Go side: zero product hits, so no half-built taxonomy survives in a JS file — and `AllIconIDs` likewise appears **nowhere** in the product or in this design's obligations. **(h) P8 `picker == Pickable`, both directions** — the invariant the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-05 names explicitly and the first draft of this obligation left implicit: the **rendered picker entries** must equal `PickableIconIDs()` — **every** pickable id appears as an option, and **no** supported-but-not-pickable id appears in the picker at all. Asserted against whatever the Slice B control actually renders (or against `ICON_PICKER`'s contents), and asserted against the existing vocabulary rather than a new one: **no third list is created** — the JS option set is compared to `PickableIconIDs()` and to the derived `ICON_PICKER`, one vocabulary with two membership flags, which is P1 seen from the picker side. P8 is P3's counterpart, and the retired-but-supported identifier is the fixture that keeps the two from being conflated: it is **absent from the picker**, while the same fixtures must show it rendering **its own glyph** on an existing card (P2/P7), still **accepted on write** (P3), and still carrying **both** its RU and EN labels (P4). A picker that hides a pickable id, offers a retired one, or drops a retired id's glyph to do it is this sub-item failing.

12. **Search matches Name **and** Description (R8/D7), and a locale switch touches no data (CD-8, CL-2):** **(a) both directions:** a query matching only the **name** finds the service, a query matching only the **description** finds it, a query matching neither excludes it, and **a service matched by both appears exactly once** — the widening is one `||` inside the single predicate at `app.js:528`, not a second pass, and a duplicate-row assertion is what proves it. **(b) composition unchanged:** the widened predicate stays AND-composed with runtime (`:529`), state (`:530`) and autostart (`:531`), and **the no-match state still renders** (`:535-542`, text `t('models.filter.no_match')`, `:539`) — an empty-description service must not become unfilterable and a non-empty query must not match a `""` description. **(c) the fold stays comparison-time only:** the query is lower-cased at `:522` and nothing normalized is ever written — assert that **no stored value changes across a search session** (phrased as a value assertion, per obligation 3's read-versus-save truth, not as a byte comparison), and that searching issues **no request at all**: it is a client-side scan over the already-loaded list (D7), so no fold can reach the file by way of the server. **(d) the icon never participates:** for every query, two services differing only by `icon_id` yield identical results, and a query equal to an icon identifier must not find a service merely because it carries that glyph — this is obligation 4's rule read from the search side, and it is the specific way an `icon_id` would become a hidden category. **(e) the locale half, carried forward from C2a:** switching RU↔EN changes **zero stored bytes** and merges nothing — set descriptions in one locale, record the file bytes, switch, re-assert; a description that **is** an existing i18n key name (`models`, `settings`) renders as that literal text, never as the key's translation (CD-9's forbidden failure mode, invisible to the composed-key blind spot at `server_test.go:73`), because user values are never passed through `t()`.

## Documentation obligations

`docs/API.md` — the two new fields on model create/update/list bodies, **and exactly two `400` diagnostics: `icon` must be a member of the built-in library (exact and case-sensitive) and `description` must be at most 2000 Unicode code points** (Q20; stated in **code points**, never bytes). Rev 3 also listed "the `400` closed-set diagnostic" here and rev 4-C2a deleted it; **the Icon-only ruling restores a vocabulary check, and the document must state five things about it, not one.** (1) **The write rule** — membership in the **supported** set, exact and case-sensitive, is the whole bound, and an id outside it is a `400` (D4). (2) **Pickability is never a write rule** — the API accepts a superset of what the picker offers (P3), so `API.md` must not describe the offered list as the accepted list, and must not imply that a value the picker no longer shows is one the API rejects. (3) **The read-side rule** — a stored unknown id is never repaired on read and renders the neutral default, while a **retired-but-supported** id renders its own glyph and is never degraded; in both cases the endpoint returns the **raw stored `icon_id`**, which the document must state is data rather than display text — an integrator that renders it into markup escapes it the way it escapes a service name (D12, obligation 10). `API.md` is where an integrator learns that a `400` on write does not imply the file will be fixed on read. (4) **The append-only rule** — an identifier already shipped is never renamed, never re-used with a different meaning and **never removed from the supported set**, so a stored id is a durable contract; retirement changes only picker availability. (5) **The value-versus-byte rule** — a repository save re-serializes the whole file (§Evidence, D8), so key order, key presence for empty values, escaping and timestamps are not stable across writes and a client must not pin bytes; what the contract guarantees is the **value** of `icon` and `description` surviving an unrelated write (obligation 3). **`API.md` documents the rules, not the membership:** the identifier list's canonical home is the built-in vocabulary itself (`internal/domain/icon.go`, surfaced to users through the picker), because copying an append-only set into prose creates a second source of truth that drifts on the first addition. ## Consequences

- The catalog arrives as **two optional fields on the entity that already exists** — one a persisted identifier drawn from a built-in library, one free text — not as an entity, and not as a taxonomy: no fourth collection, no new endpoint, no migration, no version bump, and `ARCHITECTURE.md:7`'s "4-entity domain" stays accurate. **One small domain file is added and the record should be exact about it:** rev 3 listed `internal/domain/category.go`, rev 4-C2a listed no vocabulary file at all (it stored no identifier), and the Icon-only ruling brings a vocabulary file back **for the icon** (`internal/domain/icon.go`, D6). The design's total new-file count is one, and that file is code-owned and never user-writable. CD-1 reaches FD-1's *simplification* result by a third route: rev 3 minimized surface by fixing the value set, C2a minimized it by storing no set, and rev 4 minimizes it by fixing a set **that classifies nothing**.
- **There is no organization axis in this design, and the record must say what that costs rather than quietly re-claim rev 3's argument.** Rev 3 wrote: «**The organization is code, so it cannot rot.** A closed set cannot be deleted from under a service, cannot have two members with confusingly similar names, cannot be referenced danglingly, and cannot be smuggled into a bundle or a launch.» Rev 4-C2a inverted that bullet at length; the Icon-only decision removes its subject. **What returns is rev 3's property, for the icon and for nothing else:** the icon vocabulary is code, membership is checked on write, and the library is never user-extensible, so a stored `icon_id` cannot be misspelled, cannot differ from a sibling by one letter's case, and cannot dangle by being renamed underneath a service — the three rot modes C2a's bullet enumerated are structurally impossible here, and the fourth (smuggling into a bundle or a launch) was already impossible by D11 and CD-4. **What is gone is the organization:** a catalog that outgrows its search box has no axis to group, sort or filter by, and the only remedy this design offers is R8's widened search. Because D8 forbids repair on read and §Sequencing forbids a migration, the surviving read-side hazard is exactly the one obligation 3 pins: an **unsupported** `icon_id` can still arrive — by hand-editing the durable file, by a file written by a newer version of the product, or from any other source that puts a value outside the vocabulary into storage — and it renders the neutral default while its **stored value** survives. What obligation 3 guarantees after Slice A is that **value**, not the bytes, since a save re-serializes the file (D8). **The case that no longer exists says as much as the one that does:** a supported member is never the source of such a value, because the supported set is append-only and an identifier is never removed from it (D6, P2/P7) — "the product dropped this id" is not a state this design has to handle, and an **unknown** id must never be conflated with a **retired** one, whose only difference from a pickable id is picker availability while it keeps its artwork, its labels and its write acceptance. Rev 1's D5 delete-conflict rule, D8 dangling-reference rule and Q4 uniqueness question stay deleted for FD-4's original reason — there is no category *row* to conflict with — and rev 4 does not reopen that.
- **What the user gains, and what the product now has to survive.** The user gains a glyph they choose and a description that is searchable; and, exactly as C2a promised, the right never to be blocked by their own text — a **description** can never be rejected for its content, only for its length (D4). **The icon is the one field this design does restrict**, and that restriction is the price of persisting an identifier: an unknown `icon_id` is a `400` on write. Three consequences follow the Icon-only shape rather than C2a's. **Icons are no longer best-effort:** under C2a a self-authored category was visually anonymous because only the suggestion list carried a glyph, while here **every supported value has a glyph by construction** (P2/P7) — that is what supported membership means, and the pickable subset inherits it precisely because it is a subset — and the mandatory neutral default covers only `""`, absent and unknown; since most services will carry no chosen icon for a while, the default is the most-seen glyph in the section, which is why §Sequencing 3 hands its drawing to the artifact. **The unbounded-length problem moved and shrank:** C2a's derived `<select>` had one option per distinct stored value with no ceiling, whereas the picker's option count is now **fixed by the library** and is Slice B's to choose — so the option-overflow question leaves this record with the filter, and the 64-code-point badge question never arises. **Parity coverage is real again, and its shape is two memberships rather than one list:** obligation 11 tests the **Supported** set — the persisted compatibility contract, which carries artwork coverage (P2/P7), RU and EN label coverage (P4) and write acceptance (D4, P3) — against its Go declaration, the shipped JS artwork table and both dictionaries, and tests the **Pickable** projection separately: a subset of Supported that governs **picker availability and nothing else** (P1, P3, P8). Labels sit under `models.icon.*`, a group **inside the existing `models.` prefix** (`ru.json:38-43` already holds `models.filter.*`), so no new top-level group is created and `CATALOG-DEP-06` still has no catalog instance. Against those, one thing is strictly better than rev 3 on the project's own terms: **zero migration risk**, because at HEAD `model_entry.go:7-17` stores nine fields and none of them is a category or an icon, so withdrawing the fixed vocabulary and dropping the category strands no rows.
- **A stored value can still look like the product's own identifiers, and a maintained gate notices — with the subject moved from category to description.** Rev 3's stored values were six fixed tokens, C2a's were arbitrary category strings, and the Icon-only design keeps one arbitrary field (the description) and turns the other into a checked identifier. `tests/browser/i18n.cjs:167` defines a raw-key pattern — one of eighteen top-level prefixes (`models`, `pipelines`, `runtimes`, `settings`, `wizard`, …) followed by two or more dot-separated lowercase segments — and `:183` asserts **zero** matches inside `document.body.innerText`. Verified against the committed pattern, a description a user could plausibly paste does match it: `models.filter.all`, `pipelines.btn.add` and `runtimes.delete.cascade` all trip it, while `models`, `settings`, `models.autostart` and `models.filter` do not (the regex needs a third segment, and matches substrings — so `pipelines.btn.add_model` trips it on its `pipelines.btn.add` prefix). Such a description is **legal data** under D4 and would fail a browser gate the catalog's own UI slice must pass. This record states the collision and its exact shape and does **not** resolve it: weakening that detector is a change to a maintained i18n gate and belongs to Slice B's contract or to `CATALOG-DEP-06`'s owner, never silently to a catalog slice — **and no product rule may be built from it**, because reserved prefixes, forbidden strings and content validation on the description are all outside this design (the Owner's ruling is explicit: the user's own text is not forbidden). §Test obligation 12's fixture deliberately uses **one-segment** values (`models`, `settings`) so the catalog's own test does not depend on this open question, and the `<option>` half is left unasserted here because whether a `<select>`'s option text reaches `innerText` is browser-dependent and this record has not measured it. **One half of C2a's version of this bullet dies with its subject:** an `icon_id` never appears in the DOM as text at all — the card glyph is `aria-hidden` and the picker shows a translated label (obligation **11(d)**, with the rendered-option equality of **11(h)**) — so the stored identifier cannot collide with a dictionary key in the rendered page even though identifiers themselves are lowercase dotted-free tokens.
- **R6's portable half is a documented loss, not a silent one** (D11). A migrated install keeps its services and loses their icon and description; because FD-5 chose separate transfer design rather than a bundle change, that statement belongs in `API.md`, both user guides and a new `LIMITATIONS.md` section. Rev 4's asymmetry note stays exactly where D11 put it: of the two fields, `icon_id` is the **safer** one to transfer someday — both ends ship the same vocabulary, so an arriving value can be validated on import — while free-text description transfer raises the write-path questions D11 lists. That is a reason about a future ordering, not a licence for any slice here to carry an icon across the portable boundary.
- **The sharpest consequence moved out of this ADR.** Under D5 the durable file records no signal that catalog fields exist, so an older binary discards them on the next save exactly as it already discards `pipeline_entry_id` — and a `schema_version` upper-bound guard would not have caught that case, because the case is unknown *fields*, not a higher *number*. The guard problem the rev-1 design uncovered stays registered on its own merits as `SCHEMA-VER-01` (FD-6), and this record does not claim it is solved, scheduled, or unnecessary. Its sibling is registered beside it as **`SCHEMA-VER-02`** — the fact that unknown-*field* loss already occurs between binaries that share the literal `8` (`3347b2b`, ratified as ADR 013 §D6 and pinned by `pipeline_test.go:523`) — so `SCHEMA-VER-01`'s fail-closed option is never later sold as a complete fix.
- **The secrets rule (R5) is already satisfied by the existing resolve contract**, so the ⓘ card adds no exposure *if* it consumes `environment_keys`; that is why D10 pins it to the existing endpoint instead of inventing a payload.
- **The shared-mechanism risk is now split in two, and only one half remains.** Q7 gives services their own card family, so the `.model-row` coupling with Pipelines (`CATALOG-DEP-04`) stops being a live hazard for catalog work and becomes a documented reason the pipeline files must not move; the *tooltip* coupling does not go away — the ⓘ card is required to live inside the canonical `#goaltip` system, and Q8's ruling removes the card's two existing violations while `app.js:795` keeps the pipeline one. So the residual risk in slice C is regression in a suite the slice is forbidden to edit, which is why obligation 9 names the 26 selector sites as evidence rather than as a tripwire.
- **Vocabulary is a product-surface decision, and it is now the Owner's made one.** Q17 renames the section **and its form**, so after the change the nav says «Сервисы», the wizard says «Добавить сервис» / «Название сервиса», while the **Pipeline** entry editor still says «Добавить модель» (`ru.json:82`, `pipelines.btn.add_model`), a runtime-deletion prompt still counts "модели" (`runtimes.delete.cascade`, `runtimes.delete.dependents`), and the history/instances tables keep a «Модель» column (`history.col.model`, `instances.col.model`). Inside the section, rev 3 wrote that «Q18 keeps a category called **«Модели»** living under a heading called «Сервисы»» — **kept as dated text, and now doubly no longer a design feature**: Q18's six tokens were withdrawn by C2a and the Icon-only ruling removed the field itself, so there is no built-in «Модели» category anywhere; the collision can occur only as **user text inside a description**, where it is just text on a card, and an `icon_id` cannot produce it at all because identifiers are never rendered as labels (obligation **11(d)**, and obligation 10's raw-value-is-data rule). Q17's rename and the untouched technical strings described in this bullet are **unaffected by rev 4's removal of the category**. None of that is an oversight to correct in a slice: it is the boundary the Owner drew between user-facing section vocabulary and technical terminology (Q1), and a future reviewer who "harmonizes" the remaining strings would be overruling Q17 without a decision.
- **What this design found pre-existing, and what it did not cause** — every one of these is a repository fact that stands whether or not the catalog ships, and each is registered in [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md): **CATALOG-DEP-01** (no committed home for an Owner-accepted visual contract — the blocker this ADR's §Visual contract proposes a mechanism for, and the one rev-3 ruling that did **not** dissolve: Q7 re-affirmed the artifact as mandatory), **CATALOG-DEP-02** (`ValidateCrossReferences` has zero production callers; §D8 leaves it alone), **CATALOG-DEP-03** (the card's two native-`title` violations — **now scheduled away**: Q8 rules they are fixed in the catalog's UI slice, so the debt's required direction has an owner and a slice, while `app.js:795` and the two empty `title=""` selects stay out of its scope), **CATALOG-DEP-04** (`.model-row` as one family serving two views — **resolved for services by Q7**, and it remains true for anything that later reuses the pattern, since Pipelines keep the class), and **CATALOG-DEP-06** (the raw-key detector at `tests/browser/i18n.cjs:167` allowlists **top-level prefixes** and `server_test.go:73` sees only literal `t('key')` calls, so a brand-new top-level i18n group could ship untranslated and pass both gates — which was exactly why rev 3 placed its labels under `models.category.*` rather than inventing a `categories.*` group). **Rev 4's Icon-only form follows that recorded direction for a new reason:** the icon labels live at `models.icon.<id>` — inside the existing `models.` group, reached through **literal** `t()` call sites so a missing translation is a **static** parity failure — and no category group is created at all, so the allowlist hazard still has no catalog instance. The composed-key half of the debt still matters, because what the search box matches is user data and never comes from `t()` (obligation 12). One interaction rev 3 could not have: that debt's own suggested durable fix ("any dotted `group.key.sub` token visible in the DOM that resolves to no dictionary entry is a failure") would fire on **legitimate description data** under this design too, so if `CATALOG-DEP-06` is ever closed after this ADR, its structural assertion must scope to label sites — and the value-shaped collision two bullets above is the same mechanism seen from the other side. **CATALOG-DEP-05** was promoted out to `SCHEMA-VER-01`/`-02` per FD-6 and leaves only a dated pointer.
- **Q11 buys the design an ordered world and costs it freshness.** Because nothing here may be built until ADR 018 is finished and OWNER-IMPORT-01 is closed, the catalog will be implemented against a repository whose import behavior is *already* the NEW/UPDATE/UNCHANGED/BLOCKED one — which is why D11's UPDATE-preservation is stated as a reliance instead of a request, and why §Sequencing item 0 obligates a re-read of this record's `import_plan.go` / `repository.go` / `portable.go` citations before slice A is contracted. Every line number in this record was measured at HEAD `c4241e2` on 2026-10-03; by the time the gate opens, some will have moved, and a moved citation is corrected in this file first, never argued from memory.
- Nothing here closes any open acceptance case: **OWNER-IMPORT-01**, OWNER-UX-01's formal closure and Manual Owner Acceptance remain **OPEN** (ROADMAP `:152-155`, `:164` — `:152-154` and `:163` in this record's 2026-10-03 numbering, before ADR 018's Slice 1 commit added a line to that block), ADR 015 remains **FROZEN**, and ADR 018's **Slice 1** is now IMPLEMENTED, COMMITTED and PUBLISHED (`85c7e9ca699f61668f5f441926ed09cda37bd1bd`, exact-SHA CI run `37185521486`, attempt 1, all 7 required jobs PASS) while its remaining slices stay **NOT STARTED**, so the gate Q11 imposes is still closed; nothing in this record ever edited ADR 018's bytes. **OWNER-EXPORT-01 is CLOSED** — OWNER PASS on the Owner's own `https://aids:8443` re-acceptance, recorded 2026-10-03 at ROADMAP `:162` — and this ADR neither reopens nor depends on it; ADR 019 §D27's secure-origin path is cited only as the reason a file-choosing export already exists. Under **Q11** the first three stop being merely unrelated open items and become the catalog's own start gate.

## Visual contract — how the Owner's out-of-repo mockup becomes canonical (mechanism for Q7)

R4 references «согласованный визуальный макет». On 2026-10-03 the Owner confirmed it **exists outside the repository** and asked for a mechanism to bring it in. The repository evidence is unchanged: `docs/` holds **zero** non-`.md` files (`find docs -type f ! -name "*.md"` → empty); `docs/tasks/` (3 files) and `docs/acceptance/` (3 files) are markdown-only; nothing matches `*mockup*`, `*wireframe*` or `*.canvas.tsx`; the only bitmaps are `winres/icon.png` / `winres/icon16.png` (application icon) and third-party `node_modules/` assets. AGENTS.md §Source of Truth requires an accepted *direction* to live in the canonical files, not only in chat — an accepted **visual** contract is the same class of thing.

**Rev 4 status of this section: the mechanism is still the plan, the artifact is still missing from the repository, and the artifact that exists outside it is now partly obsolete.** Q7's ruling settled the half this record could settle — services get their own card classes and Pipelines are not touched (§D9) — and re-affirmed the other half verbatim: «Визуальный макет остается обязательным перед UI-реализацией». Nothing was delivered into `docs/assets/adr020/` by the ruling, and this ADR does not invent the layout in its absence, so R4's acceptance target remains unavailable to every reviewer until the Owner supplies it by one of the three paths below. Rev 4 changes **none** of that mechanism; it changes what the artifact has to show, and it records a finding about the one that already exists.

**New finding, recorded 2026-10-04 and re-cut the same day by the Icon-only ruling: the agreed mockup is stale in exactly two regions, and both are category regions.** The Owner's out-of-repo artifact was drawn against the design rev 3 ratified, in which the filter is a closed set — a fixed number of options, each a translatable label from `models.category.*`, plus «Без категории» — and the card carries a **category badge**. Neither exists in this design: the filter's subject was withdrawn by CD-7 with CL-3/CL-4/CL-8 and then removed altogether by the Icon-only decision, and no badge is created by any slice. Whatever the mockup draws in those two regions is therefore an illustration of a design that no longer exists, and it cannot be accepted as evidence for the cards slice. **Card anatomy is otherwise not affected** — R4's compact-card layout, the ⓘ badge and the description line are rulings rev 4 leaves standing — so the artifact is stale in two regions, not withdrawn. **What the corrected artifact must now show, in the Owner's own words of 2026-10-04:** «отсутствие Category filter» (the filter row as it will actually be: search, runtime, state, autostart, and nothing else), «icon picker», «neutral/default icon», «Service card с выбранной icon», «Service card без выбранной icon», «Description / ⓘ behavior», and a «layout, который не запрещает добавить дополнительную metadata organization в будущем». **And one item is a prohibition, not a preference:** «Не добавлять сейчас пустой Category badge или placeholder» — no empty badge, no greyed-out chip, no reserved slot standing in for the future design; §Test obligation 9 asserts its absence in the rendered DOM rather than trusting the drawing. This finding is recorded here rather than resolved for three reasons: the artifact is the Owner's and this revision has no authority to redraw it; committing it per path (a) or (b) below **after** this revision means committing a known-stale reference under a hash that implies provenance, so the fact must be in the repository before that happens; and §Sequencing 3 already lists the questions the corrected version must settle. **What rev 4 does not do: lift the Q7 gate, narrow it, or accept the current artifact as satisfying it.**

**V-A (proposed) — commit two things, and bind acceptance to the second:**

1. **A pixel reference, committed as an asset:** `docs/assets/adr020/services-catalog-cards.png` (the directory does not exist yet — it would be the first non-markdown home under `docs/`). Committing a binary is demonstrably safe rather than assumed safe: tracked binaries already exist (`winres/icon.png`, `winres/icon16.png`), there is **no `.gitattributes`**, and `core.autocrlf=true` does not rewrite binary content, so a PNG introduces no line-ending hazard. Each asset gets provenance lines in the spec that references it: date received, «provided by the Owner on 2026-10-03, committed unmodified», and its `sha256` — so a later edit is detectable instead of arguable.
2. **The canonical contract itself: `docs/acceptance/ADR020-SERVICES-CATALOG-VISUAL-CONTRACT.md`**, following that directory's naming convention (`ADR011-WINDOWS-SERVICE-MANUAL-ACCEPTANCE.md`, `ADR019-HTTPS-BROWSER-ACCEPTANCE.md`). The image is *evidence*; this file is what slices are accepted against. It must state, in testable terms: card anatomy (which field on which line — the **icon slot and its glyph**, name, runtime, status, PID/uptime, action strip, ⓘ badge), geometry (grid/columns, min/max widths, behavior at 430px and at 150% zoom), the states (default / hover / ⓘ pinned / filtered-empty / **icon unset** / **icon unknown because hand-edited** / **icon retired-but-supported — a supported identifier the picker no longer offers, which must still render its own glyph, never the default** / description absent / description present), RU vs EN overflow for long names, the **card's description line** and its clipping at the field's real ceiling (2000 code points is far wider than any card, so the ⓘ is the field's home and the card shows at most a clipped line — the artifact must say which), the ⓘ card's content including the **empty-description case**, **the picker's own layout** (glyphs per row, whether labels appear beside them or only on hover, how the library's length behaves inside the control it actually uses, and what a selected entry looks like), **and the three states the draft must distinguish — unset, known-and-pickable, and stored-but-not-offered (retired or unknown) — because the round-trip contract forbids the picker from displaying a stored unknown value as if it were unset** (D6, obligation 3h), and the **neutral/default glyph as a drawn asset** — it renders for every service until the user chooses one, so it is the most-seen icon in the section and must be specified rather than improvised. **The Icon-only ruling deletes three requirements rev 4-C2a placed in this list:** the dynamic filter's overflow and per-option truncation, a card badge carrying arbitrary category text up to 64 code points including a value wider than the card, and the enumeration of degrade states for user-authored category values — none of them has a subject, and the picker's bounded option count replaces the unbounded one. **Two drafting rules, for this file as for the tests:** no V- item may name a particular icon identifier or count the library — the concrete set is a **Slice B output** (§Non-goals), so V- items describe **how an identifier renders and how the default appears** and the fixtures supply the ids; and **no V- item may reserve space for a category** — no empty badge, no placeholder slot, which is the artifact half of the Owner's «Не добавлять сейчас пустой Category badge или placeholder». Each item gets a numbered assertion **V-1…V-n** that the browser suite reproduces.

**How the Owner can supply the artifact — any one of these closes Q7's first half:**
- **(a)** attach or paste the mockup; the agent writes it to `docs/assets/adr020/`, records its `sha256`, and drafts the spec **strictly as a transcription**.
- **(b)** the Owner places the file at that path personally; the agent only references it and verifies the hash — no content authored by the agent at all.
- **(c)** if no file can be committed, the Owner dictates the measurements and states and the spec is committed as *«transcribed from the Owner's external mockup, Owner-confirmed 2026-10-03»*, with the external mockup labeled **non-authoritative** and the written spec named canonical.

**Two rules this mechanism exists to enforce.** No card acceptance may claim *"matches the mockup"* — it claims **V-k assertions pass**, because a claim about an image nobody can open is unverifiable. And the agent must not invent the contract: layout values are the Owner's product decision, so the file is written *from* the Owner's artifact or *from* dictated values.

**V-B (rejected):** keep the mockup external and record only a pointer. That reproduces the gap being closed, leaves R4 unauditable, and lets slice C be accepted on evidence no reviewer can read.

- **ADR 018's own bytes** — not edited in either direction. This revision makes that statement (cited here as `L249`, at `018-…md:265` in the current bytes) true rather than arguing about it.
- **Category, in any form** — **out of this design's scope as of 2026-10-04, and deliberately *not* banned forever** («Это НЕ означает, что Category запрещена навсегда»). The history is three-position, not one: rev 3 forbade user-defined categories "in any form"; **C2a lifted that ban as an Owner ruling** (CD-11 ordered the prohibition explicitly removed); the Icon-only decision then **removed the subject instead of re-erecting the prohibition** — no field, no filter, no suggestions, no taxonomy. **And nothing is reserved for it:** no placeholder, no badge, no spare key, no schema slot, no `if (category)` branch waiting to be filled. A real future need returns as **its own additive design revision** with storage, identity, normalization, escaping, filtering and portable questions of its own; this record neither pre-decides nor forecloses them.
- **User-supplied icon assets** — not designed here, and **excluded rather than merely unmentioned**: no uploaded images, no file paths, no URLs, no blobs, no external icon sources (D6; C2a's «Никаких пользовательских icon uploads», which survives and is now load-bearing precisely because an icon *is* persisted). Only the trusted built-in SVG library is in scope, and the neutral/default icon is **a drawn member of that library**, not something fetched when a lookup misses.
- **Transfer of the new fields to another install** — not designed here (FD-5); D11 states the local-only behavior that must be documented, and D11 records the cost list a future design inherits.
- **The forward-version storage guard** — `SCHEMA-VER-01`, per FD-6. This ADR neither adopts it, nor narrows it, nor claims the catalog is safe *because* of it; it only states (D5, Q22) that the guard would not cover the unknown-field case this design produces.
- **Which wizard step carries each input, and which control realizes the icon picker** — **not** Owner-ruled. Q20 ruled *that* the inputs live in the service form (the shared add/edit wizard, `index.html:132-211`, one modal for Create and Edit per `app.js:1242-1244`) and bounded the description at 2000 characters; its category half was withdrawn on 2026-10-04, and **the icon picker was never in that ruling**. Two choices are therefore open for slice B: Step 1 vs Step 3 of the modal (`index.html:145-150` vs `:190-210`), and native `<select>` vs a custom control — the second constrained by a platform fact rather than by taste, since a native `<option>` renders **text only** and glyph previews require a custom control (§D6, §Visual contract). §D4 records Step 1 as this design's proposal; the per-slice contract agreement is where the Owner settles both. Listed here rather than in §Questions so a future reader cannot mistake either for a ratified decision.
- **Which `icon_id` values exist** — **not** enumerated here, by the Owner's own instruction (G1's «не заменять старую fixed taxonomy новой скрытой taxonomy», carried to the library). The ADR fixes the **rules** both memberships obey — stable, append-only, never renamed or re-used for a different meaning, **never removed from the supported set**, retirement a picker flag that strips nothing, exactly one mandatory neutral/default entry, validated against the **supported** set on write — and leaves the identifiers themselves, their count, their drawings, their label wording **and which of them are pickable** to **slice B**, agreed together with the visual artifact. **Both sets are Slice B outputs; the rules they obey are this record's, as amended on 2026-10-05.** This is a scope statement for this design, **not** a permanent refusal to name them, and it is not the loose posture C2a had: because `icon_id` is persisted, the moment slice B fixes the set it becomes a **contract that cannot be edited freely afterwards** (D6 as amended 2026-10-05, D8).

This record does not decide that on the Owner's behalf; it names the ambiguity at the point where it costs a slice.
